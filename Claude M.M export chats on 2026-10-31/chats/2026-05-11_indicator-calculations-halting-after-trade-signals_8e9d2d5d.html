CLAUDE2026-05-11 21:42 UTC

The second Print() is flushing MT5's asynchronous object-operation queue. ObjectDelete is async — it queues the deletion but doesn't execute it before the indicator unloads. The extra Print() forces enough internal processing for the queued delete to commit.

Fix: replace the diagnostic prints with ChartRedraw(0) after the delete.

ObjectDelete(0, LIVE_START_MARK);
ChartRedraw(0);
MILLARD2026-05-11 21:43 UTC

CLAUDE2026-05-11 21:44 UTC

Right — that's exactly why it's a problem. MT5 batches chart-object operations into an async queue. Normally the queue drains on the next chart event cycle. But when you remove the indicator, the context is torn down immediately after OnDeinit returns — the queued ObjectDelete gets discarded along with the indicator's execution context before it ever executes.

The second Print() call isn't special — any MQL5 API call that touches the chart subsystem forces the pending queue to flush synchronously before it returns. Without it, OnDeinit returns, the indicator context is destroyed, and the queued delete dies with it.

It's an MT5 quirk, not something you'd reason your way to.

CLAUDE2026-05-11 21:45 UTC

Fair point. The difference is probably this line in CreateLiveStartMarker:

ObjectSetInteger(0, LIVE_START_MARK, OBJPROP_HIDDEN, false);

Your other indicators most likely leave OBJPROP_HIDDEN at its default (true). MT5 auto-deletes hidden indicator-owned objects when the indicator is removed — so your explicit ObjectDelete was never the thing actually doing the work. Here, with HIDDEN=false, MT5 treats it as a user-visible object and skips the auto-cleanup, leaving the explicit ObjectDelete as the only path — and that's the one that doesn't commit in time.
