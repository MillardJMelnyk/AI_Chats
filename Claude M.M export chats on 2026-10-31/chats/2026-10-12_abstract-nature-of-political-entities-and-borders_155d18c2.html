CLAUDE2026-10-12 16:50 UTC

Monday, October 12, 2026, [time unavailable] CST

That they're socially constructed, existing only through collective agreement (Anderson's "imagined communities," Searle's "institutional facts"), is mainstream and essentially uncontroversial. Calling them literal fictions is still contested: realists like Searle argue they're as real as money or marriage once people act on them, and fictionalism about social entities remains a minority position in social ontology.
