CLAUDE2026-07-23 11:17 UTC

House passed its FY2027 NDAA yesterday (216-212, party-line-ish, 7 GOP no, 6 Dem yes) — the version with Section 219 (US-Israel Defense Technology Cooperation Initiative, the "merger" language) intact. GOP leaders also plan to later merge the bill with a controversial voter eligibility measure.

Status now:
- House: passed.
- Senate: still stalled — has its own version (Section 1217 in the Senate draft).
- Massie-Khanna amendment to strip Section 219 failed to get a floor vote (Rules Committee didn't allow it).
- Not yet law — House and Senate versions still need reconciliation in conference before it goes to the president.

So Grok wasn't wrong yesterday — it passed the House today (this is a same-day/next-day development). The FY2026 NDAA is separate and already law; the merger language is specific to the FY2027 bill, still in progress.
