February 2026 Security Event (Now Resolved)

A significant security situation unfolded in late February. On February 22, 2026, a widespread alert was issued covering multiple states, including Jalisco (Puerto Vallarta, Guadalajara), Baja California (Tijuana, Ensenada), and Quintana Roo (Cancún, Cozumel, Playa del Carmen, Tulum).

State-Level Advisories

The risk is not uniform across Mexico:

  • The highest-concern areas include Sinaloa and Zacatecas, which carry Level 4 "Do Not Travel" designations. Yucatán and Campeche carry no warning.
  • Several states have been elevated to Level 3 – Reconsider Travel, including Jalisco (home to Guadalajara and Puerto Vallarta) and Baja California (including Tijuana and Ensenada), due to an uptick in violent crimes, particularly in outlying areas.
  • Six Mexican states in total carry Level 4 "Do Not Travel" warnings — the same severity level assigned to active conflict zones.

Key Recommendations from the State Department

Travelers are advised to check the latest advisories, enroll in the Smart Traveler Enrollment Program (STEP) for alerts, stay in tourist areas, and keep family members informed of their itinerary.

You can check the full, up-to-date state-by-state breakdown at travel.state.gov.
