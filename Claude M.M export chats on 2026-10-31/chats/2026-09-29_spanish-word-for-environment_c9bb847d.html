I just bought a fridge here in Mexico and this came with it. What does it mean?

CLAUDE2026-09-29 03:06 UTC

It's Mexico's mandatory energy label: your fridge uses about 321 kWh a year (roughly 27 kWh a month), just under the legal maximum of 325, so it's only 1% more efficient than the minimum the law allows.

Line by line:
- Eficiencia Energética: Energy efficiency, rated under Mexico's official standard NOM-015-ENER-2018.
- Refrigerador - Congelador: Fridge-freezer.
- Marca / Modelo: Brand RCA, model BCD-283W.
- Capacidad: 306 liters.
- Sistema de deshielo: Defrost system, automatic, long-cycle.
- Consumo de Energía: Energy use, 321 kWh per year.
- Límite de Consumo: The legal maximum for this size and type, 325 kWh per year.
- Ahorro de Energía: Energy savings versus that limit, 1% on a scale of 0 to 50%.
- Importante: Real use depends on how you use it; the label can't be removed until a customer buys it (so you can toss it now); compare with similar models before buying.
