is there a key in path of exile to highlight items and interactables?

CLAUDE2026-09-25 19:34 UTC

Yes — hold Alt to highlight all items in your vicinity, including hidden chests and items; Z toggles item labels on permanently.

MILLARD2026-09-25 19:47 UTC

WHY WOULD I WANT TO LOCKA GEMS LEVEL AND NOT LEVEL IT UP WHEN POSSIBLE IN PATH OF EXILE?

CLAUDE2026-09-25 19:47 UTC

Mainly because a higher level raises the gem's attribute requirement (Str/Dex/Int) and its mana or spirit cost, so you hold it back when you can't meet the requirement or can't afford the cost, or when you're keeping a triggered setup like Cast when Damage Taken low enough to still fire.

MILLARD2026-09-25 19:54 UTC

how can i see the cost and requirement?

CLAUDE2026-09-25 19:54 UTC

Hover your mouse over the gem (in your inventory, socket, or skill bar) and its tooltip lists the mana/spirit cost and the Str/Dex/Int requirement.
