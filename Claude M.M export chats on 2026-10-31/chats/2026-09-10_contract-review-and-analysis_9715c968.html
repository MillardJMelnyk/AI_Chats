Clauses that can bite you

Clause 13 — the worst one. The $16,600 penalty is payable "por cada incumplimiento" — for each breach of any clause. Read literally, paying a water bill late twice costs you $33,200. Push to have this limited to early termination and to material breaches only.

Clause 9 — utilities. You pay electricity, water, gas, phone, and must hand over the original paid receipts. If you don't, the rent counts as unpaid, and late payment or suspension of any service is grounds for rescission. Keep every receipt and email them to her monthly.

Clause 16 — rescission triggers you don't control. A written complaint from two neighbors (E) or from the administrator (F) can end your lease. Clause 5 adds: more than three administration sanctions during the term also ends it. Entirely subjective.

Clause 6 Bis — 48-hour reporting window. You must notify defects in writing within 48 hours of noticing them or you pay for the consequences. She then has 15 calendar days to start repairs, immediate for genuine emergencies. Report everything in writing, immediately, even minor things.

Clause 8 — fire, flood, or similar. If a siniestro happens in the unit, you owe the landlord and the neighbors for damages, with no fault requirement written in. Get renters' liability insurance (responsabilidad civil). The contract allows it and names her as beneficiary for liability; theft coverage stays yours.

Clause 14 — póliza jurídica. On renewal you must renew a legal policy with ACCR at your cost, before expiry, or it's grounds for rescission. The contract never states the price. Get that number in writing now.

Clause 7 — landlord access. You must allow entry for inspection and repair. No advance-notice requirement anywhere. Ask for 24 hours' notice except emergencies.

Clause 3 — rent accrues by whole months regardless of when you vacate.

Clause 2 — 12 months forced on both sides. No extension, no automatic renewal. To renew or to leave, either side must give notice by email at least 30 days before 14 Sep 2027. Put that date in your calendar for mid-August.

Obligado solidario

Claudia Solorzano Plata is jointly and severally liable for everything, waives the benefits of order, exclusion and division, and stays liable through any extension or rent change without being asked. Her liability ends only when the landlord formally receives the unit empty and debt-free. Confirm she is actually your intended co-signer — given the tenant block is someone else's, hers may be too.

At signing

  • Initial every page. Four copies are executed; take one fully signed original home.
  • Photograph and video the entire unit on handover day, timestamped, and email the set to the landlord the same day with the inventory. That email is what protects your $8,300.
  • Confirm the 2 parking spaces are identified by number somewhere.
  • Pets are permitted, with damage from pets explicitly excluded from normal wear.

I'm not a lawyer and this isn't legal advice — it's a read of what the document says. Given the wrong-tenant problem alone, send it back to ACCR before anything else.
