MILLARD2026-08-10 15:24 UTC

what order book and other level II data is available in cTrader?

CLAUDE2026-08-10 15:24 UTC

Two ways to get it, depending which layer you're coding against:

cAlgo/cTrader Automate (C#): MarketData.GetMarketDepth(Symbol) returns a MarketDepth object with BidEntries and AskEntries (price+volume at each level), plus an Updated event you subscribe to for live changes. This is broker-liquidity depth, not exchange order book — reflects what your broker's LPs are quoting.

Open API (protobuf, cross-language): subscribe with ProtoOASubscribeDepthQuotesReq (needs ctidTraderAccountId + symbolId), then you get ProtoOADepthEvent messages with newQuotes/deletedQuotes fields — prices need dividing by 100000 and rounding to symbol digits. Unsubscribe via ProtoOAUnsubscribeDepthQuotesReq.

No time-and-sales/tape or full exchange-level order book beyond what your broker exposes — it's Level II depth (bid/ask ladder with volume per level), not raw order-by-order data.
