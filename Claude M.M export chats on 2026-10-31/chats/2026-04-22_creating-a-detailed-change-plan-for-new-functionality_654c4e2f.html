The repeating structural unit is a tested-and-rejected line event. Each event has these attributes:

  • Which line was tested (lower outer, midline, upper outer)

  • Outcome: rejected (price came back), or breached (price stayed)
  • MA follow-through: did any MA cross with price, did any stay across

Multiple of these events accumulate into a rejection cluster at a line. The cluster has:

  • Which line
  • Which direction the rejections are happening from
  • How many tests
  • Whether any MA managed to cross at any point
