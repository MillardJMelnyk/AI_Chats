Yes, buildable in cTrader. Read your MQL5 file — half-circle gauge, win %, total/win/loss, realized + floating P/L, last N closed deals, minimize button, draggable with saved position, chart symbol only, from a start date.

Ambiguities I won't decide for you:

  1. Host. cTrader "Plugin" ≠ MT5 chart overlay. Options: ASP block (docked side panel), Trade Watch tab, floating window, custom chart frame, or a chart Indicator (closest to what you have now — sits on the chart, follows chart symbol). You said plugin; your MT5 version is an indicator.
  2. Rendering. Native cTrader controls give you text and boxes but no arc — the dial would be a bar, not a gauge. A real gauge needs WebView (Edge WebView2, HTML/SVG, .NET 6+ algo). WebView is how it gets "better" looking; it's also heavier and desktop-only.
  3. Symbol scope. Chart symbol automatically, a dropdown to pick the pair, or all symbols with a per-symbol breakdown.
  4. Win definition. cTrader closed trades give NetProfit (after commission and swap) and GrossProfit. Yours used net. Also, partial closes count as separate closed trades in cTrader same as MT5 deals — each partial counts as its own win or loss unless I group by position ID.
  5. History depth. cTrader only exposes what the client has loaded for the account, so a start date earlier than the loaded range silently gives you fewer trades. Fixed start date input, or rolling window (last 30 days / last N trades)?
  6. Recent trades row content. Yours shows time + P/L. Add direction, volume, entry/exit, duration, R multiple?

Answer those and I'll give you the exact build plan before writing anything.

can you also add like a percent of trades that hit tp and percent that hit sl?

CLAUDE2026-08-03 19:05 UTC

Not available from history. cTrader exposes the close reason only in the Positions.Closed event, live — HistoricalTrade carries no reason, no stop loss, no take profit. So a TP/SL split over your existing trades cannot be computed at all.

What is possible: record the reason as trades close while the indicator is running, write it to the indicator's data folder, and show percentages over that recorded set. It starts empty and only ever covers trades closed while a chart with the indicator is open — miss a session and those trades are uncounted forever. It also needs AccessRights.FullAccess.

Your call: build that, or drop it.
