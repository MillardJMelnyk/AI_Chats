The only way to acquire evidence that supports a claim is to have prior knowledge that was used to create the claim in the first place. That knowledge pre-exists all evidence, because evidence can't be identified without it. "There is no evidence" leaves open that someone might find some, but the regress shows that's impossible: any evidence for the link between evidence and claim would itself require a further unevidenced link, and that one another, without end.
