The word "spectacles" can refer to:

  1. Eyeglasses - the optical devices worn to correct vision (this is the more common usage in British English, while Americans typically say "glasses")

  2. Public displays or performances - impressive shows or events that are meant to be watched, like circus performances, parades, or other grand exhibitions
