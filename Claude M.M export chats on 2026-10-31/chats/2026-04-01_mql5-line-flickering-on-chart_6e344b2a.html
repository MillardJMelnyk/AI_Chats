CLAUDE2026-04-01 23:17 UTC

Three distinct issues causing the flicker. Here's what's happening:

1. The last leg's pivot is never locked — it floats every tick (main flicker source)

In the main loop, the current (unconfirmed) pivot is written to ZZ[] and tracked on every tick. On the next call, ClearRecordedPivots() wipes all tracked bars including that live tip pivot, then the loop rewrites it — potentially at a different price. This is the classic ZigZag repaint: the trailing end of the line moves on every tick because nothing distinguishes "confirmed pivot" from "candidate pivot."

The standard fix: don't write the current open leg's tip to the buffer at all, or write it only to a separate buffer that isn't plotted. Only commit a pivot to ZZ[] when the opposite-direction leg has confirmed it by meeting InpMinSpread + InpMinPeriods.

2. g_TouchedBars[] double-tracks the live pivot, inflating on every tick

When the live pivot extends (same-direction extension branch, lines 328–334), you call TrackTouchedBar(pivotBar) after the new bar is set. That's correct. But the old bar was already tracked when it was first written. So you accumulate stale entries in g_TouchedBars[] for bars that were cleared — they get re-cleared harmlessly on the next call but the array grows unboundedly on a busy tick stream. Not the flicker cause directly, but it's unnecessary work and will bloat memory on a fast chart.

3. Full reset triggered too aggressively

The reset condition:

prev_calculated==0 || g_LastRatesTotal<=0 || rates_total<g_LastRatesTotal || g_FirstBarTime!=time[0]

The g_FirstBarTime!=time[0] check is fine. But rates_total<g_LastRatesTotal will fire on any history reload or broker-side bar removal, forcing a full ArrayInitialize pass over potentially tens of thousands of bars. That's not flicker per se, but it causes a full visual redraw that looks like a flash/jump. It's also unnecessary since the incremental path handles new bars correctly — you could tighten this to only reset on prev_calculated==0.

Summary of what to fix:

Issue Fix
Live tip repaints every tick Don't store the unconfirmed leg tip in ZZ[]; only commit pivots once the reversal leg confirms them
g_TouchedBars grows on extensions On pivot extension, remove the old bar index before adding the new one, or just accept the harmless redundancy
Over-aggressive full reset Simplify reset condition to prev_calculated==0 \|\| g_FirstBarTime!=time[0]

The first issue is the flicker. The third is the flash-on-reload. The second is a slow memory leak.
