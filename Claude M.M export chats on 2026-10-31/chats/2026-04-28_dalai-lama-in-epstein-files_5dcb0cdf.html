All references were determined to be strictly third-party mentions — informal, speculative, or contextual — with no direct interaction, relationship, or communication established between the Dalai Lama and Epstein.

The conclusion: Epstein was actively seeking a meeting with the Dalai Lama but the records show he never achieved it.

AFP's own search of the files produced 154 results but found no mention or suggestion of an actual meeting between the two.

The controversy was amplified by Chinese state-affiliated media outlets shortly after the files' release, coinciding with the Dalai Lama receiving a Grammy Award — timing consistent with past Chinese government reactions when Tibetan leadership receives international recognition.

Bottom line: his name is in there, but solely as someone Epstein wanted access to. No evidence of a meeting, relationship, or any awareness on the Dalai Lama's part of Epstein's activities.
