Forehead + ear pressure for 2-3 days points at sinus congestion — the frontal sinuses sit behind the forehead and the eustachian tubes feed the ears, so both block together. Tension headache is the other common cause but doesn't usually give ear pressure.

What helps:
- Saline nasal rinse or spray, several times a day
- Oxymetazoline spray (Afrin/Iliadin) — max 3 days, longer causes rebound congestion
- Ibuprofen or paracetamol for the pain
- Steam, hydration

Get it looked at if: fever over 38.5°C, one-sided face or tooth pain, pain worse leaning forward for more than 10 days, green discharge with fever, vision changes, stiff neck, or the worst headache of your life.

If it's still there in a week or getting worse instead of better, see a doctor rather than keep self-treating.
