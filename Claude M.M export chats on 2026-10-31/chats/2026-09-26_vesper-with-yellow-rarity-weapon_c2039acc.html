in path of exile, if i already have a yellow rarity weapon, will putting it in the vesper be a bad idea?

CLAUDE2026-09-26 00:25 UTC

No, it's not a bad idea: Vesper works on rares, and a failed craft never destroys the item; it just burns the Sulphur and shows you one outcome instead of several (Likely, based on 3.29 guides, not the official patch notes).

The two ways it can still hurt you: every option still comes from the orb you feed in, so a Chaos Orb rerolls the whole weapon on every copy and you could end up worse than what you started with. And the only thing in Vesper that can delete the item is Tzamoto's Ducat, which has a 50% chance to remove all Intangibility and a 50% chance to destroy the item entirely.
