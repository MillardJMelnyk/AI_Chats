Is there an acceptable dose of vitamin c for 5 months old babies who are coming down with the sniffles

CLAUDE2026-10-14 16:10 UTC

Wednesday, October 14, 2026, 10:10 AM CST

No. The daily need at 5 months is about 40 mg, which breast milk or formula already covers, and there's no recommended supplemental dose for colds at that age; extra vitamin C hasn't been shown to shorten or ease a cold in babies.
