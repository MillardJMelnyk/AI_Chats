Yes, if "Nazi" means someone who held the ideology, since most historians of fascism (Paxton, Griffin, Payne) classify Nazism as a form of fascism, with a minority (notably Zeev Sternhell) treating its racial core as a separate category. If "Nazi" means anyone who carried a party card, it's less clean: many of the roughly 8.5 million members joined for careers, jobs, or self-protection rather than conviction.
