close[] vs (bid+ask)/2.

On the live bar at any tick: close[rates_total - 1] equals the last trade price, which on most MT5 brokers is the current bid. Bid and ask differ by the spread. Mid = (bid + ask) / 2 sits half a spread above the bid.

So close[live] is offset from mid by roughly half the spread, always on the bid side. On XAUUSD M1 that's typically 20-50 cents depending on broker and time of day. It moves with every tick the same way mid does, but shifted.

For your AGGR logic — channel crossings, threshold crossings — using close[] instead of mid means every crossing test is being evaluated against a price that's systematically half-a-spread below the true mid. The lines (LWMAs of bar highs/lows/body-medians) are computed from close-style data too, so there's some self-consistency, but the offset is real.
