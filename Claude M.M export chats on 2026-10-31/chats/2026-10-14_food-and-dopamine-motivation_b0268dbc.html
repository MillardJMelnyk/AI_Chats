MILLARD2026-10-14 15:04 UTC

Explain nothing buttery

CLAUDE2026-10-14 15:04 UTC

It's a term coined by British neuroscientist Donald MacKay for claims of the form "X is nothing but Y": love is nothing but chemicals, a person is nothing but atoms, scrolling is nothing but dopamine. The error is taking a true description at one level and treating it as if it cancels every other level, like saying a letter from your mother is nothing but ink on paper: accurate about the ink, and blind to the only thing that makes it a letter.

MILLARD2026-10-14 15:04 UTC

But why buttery?

CLAUDE2026-10-14 15:04 UTC

It's a pun: "nothing but" plus "-ery," spelled to sound like butter, as a mocking label for the habit of saying "nothing but."
