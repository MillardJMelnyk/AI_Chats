CLAUDE2026-08-31 18:08 UTC

Yes — cash works in the CFEmáticos, but only between 8:00 and 16:00; outside those hours the machines take cards only.

CLAUDE2026-08-31 18:18 UTC

Press 0 for a live agent — that's the option you want, and the 071 menu doesn't reliably map to what you're doing beyond that.

You're standing in a CFE office. Skip the phone and hand your receipt to someone at the counter instead — faster, and gestures work.
