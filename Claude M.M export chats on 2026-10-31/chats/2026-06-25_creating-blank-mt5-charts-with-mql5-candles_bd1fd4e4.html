Can a chart be created in MT5 as a blank canvas -- no data for an instrument displayed -- then populated by the MQL5 function that creates candles?

CLAUDE2026-06-25 15:25 UTC

Yes.

ChartOpen() will open a chart window for a symbol/period, but it still pulls and renders that symbol's real price history — you can't get a chart window with literally nothing behind it that way.

What you actually want is a custom symbol. You create one (via SymbolCreate() or manually in Market Watch → right-click → "Symbols" → custom symbol), give it no bars, then open a chart on that custom symbol — it'll be blank. Then you feed it candles yourself with CustomRatesUpdate() (or CustomRatesReplace() for bulk), and the chart renders whatever OHLC data you push, bar by bar, in real time or backfilled.

So the pipeline is:
1. SymbolCreate(name, path) — define the custom symbol
2. SymbolExist() check / CustomSymbolSetInteger/Double/String for specs (digits, point, etc.)
3. ChartOpen(name, period) — opens blank since no rates exist yet
4. CustomRatesUpdate(name, &rates[]) — push bars in, chart draws them as you go

This is the standard approach for synthetic instruments, replay tools, and exactly the kind of mock/tick-replay visualization work you've been doing in Python — except this does it natively in MT5's own chart engine instead of matplotlib.
