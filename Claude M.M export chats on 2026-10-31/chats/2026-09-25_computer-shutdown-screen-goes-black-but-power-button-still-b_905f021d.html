why when i chose to shutdown my computer form the startbar does it go black like it has shut down but the power button gently blinks off an on

CLAUDE2026-09-25 18:01 UTC

A slowly pulsing power LED means the PC went to sleep, not shutdown. Likely.

To confirm, press the power button once: if you land straight back on your desktop with your windows still open, it was asleep; if it goes through a full boot (motherboard logo, boot menu), it really was off and that blink is just your motherboard's standby light.

i press shutdown and it goes to sleep, why?

CLAUDE2026-09-25 18:02 UTC

I can't tell why without seeing what Windows actually logged when you clicked it.

Right after it happens, wake it, then right-click Start → Terminal (Admin), paste this, and send me the output:

wevtutil qe System /q:"*[System[(EventID=42 or EventID=13 or EventID=109 or EventID=1074)]]" /c:6 /rd:true /f:text

That shows whether Windows recorded a real shutdown or a sleep, and what triggered it.
