CLAUDE2026-05-18 01:35 UTC

In the early part of the story, Yuru is told only that Asa has a mysterious "duty" to perform on behalf of the village — she's locked in a cell at the heart of the village's central building, and no one will explain why. She has to live in a prison in the village's central building and can never leave.

The real reason is revealed once the village is attacked and the eye-patched invader claiming to be the "real Asa" shows up. According to Asa, Grandma created a fake Asa in order to keep Yuru tethered to the village after their parents fled with the real one. The "Asa" Yuru has been visiting in the cell his whole life isn't actually his sister — she's a constructed double, kept caged because Yuru's emotional attachment to her is what keeps him from ever leaving the village to look for his real family. The cage isn't punishment; it's the mechanism that anchors the lie.

A lingering open question at that point in the story is why the parents took the real Asa and left Yuru behind — whether by choice or necessity isn't yet clear.
