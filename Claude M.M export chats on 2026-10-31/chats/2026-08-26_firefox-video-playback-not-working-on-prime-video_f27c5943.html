I was watching a video on Prime Video and suddenly it stopped and displayed this. No video will play now. I use Firefox. Edge works fine. I've cleared the Firefox cache. I've changed DNS settings. How do I get Firefox to play videos again?

CLAUDE2026-08-26 23:11 UTC

Re-install Firefox's Widevine DRM plugin — that's what Prime Video uses and it's broken in your profile.

  1. Firefox → Settings → General → scroll to Digital Rights Management (DRM) Content → uncheck Play DRM-controlled content, close Firefox.
  2. Open %APPDATA%\Mozilla\Firefox\Profiles\<your profile>\gmp-widevinecdm and delete the whole folder.
  3. Start Firefox, re-check Play DRM-controlled content, wait for it to download the plugin (check Settings → Add-ons → Plugins shows Widevine enabled/"Always Activate").
  4. Reload Prime Video.

If it still fails, disable hardware acceleration (Settings → General → Performance → uncheck Use recommended performance settings → uncheck Use hardware acceleration when available) and restart.
