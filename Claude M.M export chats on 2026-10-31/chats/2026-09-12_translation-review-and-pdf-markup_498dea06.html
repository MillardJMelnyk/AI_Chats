OBJECTIONS — negotiate

Clause 2 (Term). Internally contradictory: the term "concluye el día prefijado sin necesidad de desahucio" — ends automatically, no notice needed — and then two paragraphs later requires both parties to give 30 days' written notice stating whether they renew or terminate. Get it stated that failure to give notice does not extend the term, does not renew, and triggers no penalty. Holdover is $1,000/day plus rent; ask for it capped.

Clause 3 (Rent). Payment window is the 15th–19th only — five days. The late-charge paragraph then refers to "la fecha de pago" without defining it. Define: due date = the 19th, late charges run from the 20th. The charges themselves are 5% of a full month's rent if paid within five days, 10% after — flat penalties, not interest. 10% of $8,300 is $830 for being six days late. Push for a single 5% tier. Separately, the sentence imposing an annual INPC increase is inoperative inside a fixed 12-month term; it belongs only in the renewal clause — have it deleted here. Verify the CLABE belongs to Laura Daniela Gomez Meza before the first transfer.

Arithmetic checks out: 12 × $8,300 = $99,600; penalty 2 × $8,300 = $16,600; deposit $8,300.

Clause 3, final sentence. Rent may not be withheld "por ningún concepto o determinación, ya sea judicial o extrajudicial" — no set-off even against a court order. Ask for removal, or at minimum subordination to the repair exception now in Clause 13.

Clause 4 (Use). Uncapped indemnity for the full sale value of the property under the Extinción de Dominio law, triggered by acts of anyone the tenant admits to the premises. Limit to acts imputable to the tenant.

Clause 5 (Rules). Three sanctions during the term = rescission, with no right to see the complaint, contest the fine, or cure. Demand written notice of each sanction and a right to contest before it counts toward the three.

Clause 6 BIS (Hidden defects). Kelly must report any defect within 48 hours of detecting it or pay damages for the delay. That is unreasonably tight — a slow leak noticed on a Friday. Change to "promptly, and in any event within 5 business days." It also contradicts Clause 7, which uses the looser "oportunamente" standard for the same duty; align them. And the landlord only has to begin repairs within 15 days — there is no completion deadline anywhere. Add an outside date and a repair-and-deduct remedy.
