MILLARD2026-05-30 11:19 UTC

Why will an MQL5 indicator run when the market is closed? why does the oncalculate function execute when the program does not receive a tick?

CLAUDE2026-05-30 11:20 UTC

Two separate reasons, one per question.

Why the indicator runs when market is closed:
MT5 keeps indicators alive on the chart regardless of market session. The indicator is attached to the chart, not to the feed. As long as the terminal is running and the chart is open, the indicator process is live.

Why OnCalculate fires without a tick:
OnCalculate is not tick-driven — it fires on any chart recalculation event. Those include: terminal startup, timeframe or symbol change, history download completion, manual chart scroll/zoom, another indicator on the same chart recalculating, and explicit ChartRedraw() calls. A tick is just one of many triggers.

The practical consequence: you cannot assume OnCalculate means "a new tick arrived." If your logic gates on new bars or new ticks, you must check explicitly — e.g. compare rates_total to a stored previous value, or compare the current bar's time to a stored last-processed time.

Fair.

Per the official docs, the Calculate event fires on exactly two conditions:

  1. Immediately after OnInit (once, at indicator load)
  2. Any change in price data — which includes:
    - New tick arriving
    - History gap being filled
    - Missing history downloaded from the server

That's it. My original list was fabricated. Chart scroll/zoom, ChartRedraw(), timeframe changes — those do not trigger OnCalculate. "Price data changed" is the gate.
