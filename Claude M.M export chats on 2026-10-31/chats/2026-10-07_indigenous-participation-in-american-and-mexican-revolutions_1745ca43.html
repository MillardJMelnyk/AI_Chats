The 1910 revolution's armies, especially Zapata's, were heavily indigenous and mestizo peasants fighting for land, while the leadership that won (Madero, Carranza, Obregón) was still mostly criollo and mestizo elite. If you mean the 1810 independence war, which is the closer analog to the American one, the same holds even more strongly: Hidalgo's early army was largely indigenous.
