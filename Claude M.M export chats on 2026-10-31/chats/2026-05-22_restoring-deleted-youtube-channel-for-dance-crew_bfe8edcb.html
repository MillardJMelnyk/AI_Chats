Here's what they should try, in order:

1. YouTube Appeal Form (first move)
Go to support.google.com/youtube/troubleshooter/2968491 — this is the official channel termination appeal. Submit it explaining it was a verification issue, not a policy violation.

2. Google Account Recovery
If the underlying Google account was also affected, try accounts.google.com/signin/recovery first to restore account access before appealing the channel.

3. YouTube Help Community
Post at support.google.com/youtube/community — volunteer "Diamond" experts there sometimes have escalation paths regular users don't.

4. Twitter/X — @TeamYouTube
Publicly tweet at @TeamYouTube with a brief, polite description. This genuinely works faster than most official channels. Keep it factual and non-confrontational.

5. Gather evidence now
Before anything else, collect: channel URL, channel ID (from old links), any emails from Google, screenshots, upload dates of videos. This speeds up any appeal.

Key point to state in the appeal: The termination was due to a verification issue, not a Terms of Service violation — that distinction matters and may fast-track restoration.

Near-2k subscribers with original content and no policy violations is a strong case. Worth being persistent with multiple appeals if the first is denied.
