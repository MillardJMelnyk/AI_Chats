i want to think of some easy questions to ask my student to help us later reflect on the stories she gives with th emodal perfect. For example one question could be tell me about a time you created an issue at work and what you could have done differently?

CLAUDE2026-07-29 22:45 UTC

Work
- Tell me about a time you made a mistake at work.
- Tell me about a time you had a problem with a coworker.
- Tell me about a time you missed a deadline.
- Tell me about a time you said yes to too much work.
- Tell me about a time you got bad news from a boss or a client.

Money / decisions
- Tell me about something you bought and regretted.
- Tell me about a time you spent too much money.
- Tell me about a decision you thought about for a long time.

People
- Tell me about a time you argued with a friend or family member.
- Tell me about a time you forgot something important.
- Tell me about a time you hurt someone's feelings by accident.
- Tell me about a time you didn't say something you wanted to say.

Travel / daily life
- Tell me about a time you were late for something important.
- Tell me about a trip that went wrong.
- Tell me about a time you got lost.
- Tell me about a time you lost something.

School / learning
- Tell me about a test or class that went badly.
- Tell me about something you started and didn't finish.
- Tell me about a time you didn't study enough.

Reflection follow-ups (same three, every story)
- What could you have done differently?
- What should you have done?
- What would you do now?

Modal perfect is B1 grammar, above the A2 ceiling in your curriculum map.
