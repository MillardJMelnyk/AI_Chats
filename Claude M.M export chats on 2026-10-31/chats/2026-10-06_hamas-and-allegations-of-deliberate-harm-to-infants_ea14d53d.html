What's the evidence that Hamas killed babies deliberately?

CLAUDE2026-10-06 15:04 UTC

Tuesday, October 6, 2026, 9:03 AM CST

Thin: the "40 beheaded babies" and fetus-cut-from-womb stories were never substantiated (Haaretz debunked the fetus claim), and Israel's own Social Security data, per Haaretz, shows one infant killed on Oct 7 (10-month-old Mila Cohen, Nir Oz), with no public evidence she was targeted rather than killed in the assault. The only "deliberate" case is the IDF's forensic claim that Kfir (9 months) and Ariel Bibas were killed by hand in captivity in Nov 2023. Hamas says an Israeli airstrike killed them, and no independent autopsy exists, so it's unresolved; a deliberate pattern of baby-killing is Highly unlikely on the available evidence.

CLAUDE2026-10-06 15:07 UTC

Roughly 20,500 on Gaza Health Ministry counts: the Ministry's detailed figures on October 7, 2025 listed 20,179 children killed, 30 percent of 67,173 total, and UNICEF says another 265 children have been killed since the October 2025 ceasefire. Demographic-modelling estimates that include the uncounted dead put it at roughly 19,000 to 34,000 child deaths, which excludes children killed by Israel in the West Bank and Lebanon.
