does rocket league change the velocity of gameplay when they update it?

CLAUDE2026-08-01 05:43 UTC

Rarely, and not on purpose.

Psyonix treats the core physics (ball bounce, car acceleration, boost, gravity) as sacred and leaves the numbers alone across most patches. The real exceptions have been deliberate structural changes — the 2018 hitbox standardization into six presets, early-season ball friction/bounce tweaks — plus occasional unintended physics regressions after an engine or netcode update, which players spot within hours and Psyonix reverts.

What does shift more often is perceived pace: server tick behavior, input latency, frame-rate handling, and matchmaking. Those change how the game feels without touching a single physics constant, which is why "the ball feels different this patch" is usually right in sensation and wrong in cause.

If you mean literal velocity caps — ball max speed and car max speed have been unchanged for years.
