CLAUDE2026-07-22 19:58 UTC

Read the file. Here's what's actually possible in MT5 for a canvas-based bitmap panel:

  • True click-drag resize (corner handle): possible, but you have to hand-roll it — track mouse down/drag on a small hit-zone in the corner, recompute canvas width/height, redraw. Real complexity, real edge cases (min size, redraw cost every frame while dragging).
  • Scroll wheel inside the panel: not possible. MT5 doesn't deliver mouse wheel events to chart objects/indicators — no hook exists for it. Scrolling would have to be up/down arrow buttons instead, not wheel.
  • Minimize toggle: straightforward — a small clickable region that collapses the panel to just the dial (or just a title bar), redraws smaller canvas.
  • Compact layout / smaller dial: straightforward — shrink dial radius, tighten line spacing and font sizes, fits current 3 sections without cutoff at typical InpRecentTrades counts.

So "resize + scroll" as literally described is the expensive, bug-prone route. "Minimize + compact" gets you the actual outcome (nothing cut off, panel not always taking up a huge footprint) with far less risk.

Which do you want:

A. Minimize button + smaller dial + tighter spacing (no drag-resize, no scroll) — recommended
B. Full drag-resize corner handle + up/down scroll buttons for recent trades (no wheel)
C. Both A and B

Say which, then GO.

CLAUDE2026-07-22 21:19 UTC

Selling channels:

  1. MQL5 Market — register as Seller on MQL5.community (ID verification required), publish product with a demo version, buyers pay through MQL5's internal payment system. This is the standard route and gets you in front of the built-in MT5 user base.
  2. Outside MQL5: your own site/Gumroad/Discord — sell the .ex5 (compiled) directly, handle licensing yourself (harder to enforce, no built-in trial/payment infra).

Price: Dashboard/utility indicators like this — display-only, no signals, no entries — sit at the low end of the market. Comparable info panels (P/L calendars, trade-stat dashboards) go for roughly $15–40. Indicators claiming actual trade signals or "edge" price much higher ($50–200+), but those are a different category and mostly marketing — yours is read-only reporting, not a signal generator, so pricing it like a signal tool would be dishonest to buyers.

Realistic range for this as-is: $15–30. To justify more you'd need to add something with actual decision value (per-session breakdown, drawdown tracking, multi-symbol support) — not just visual polish.
