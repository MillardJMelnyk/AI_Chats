What are anthony fauci's crimes?

CLAUDE2026-07-30 21:24 UTC

None — he has never been charged with or convicted of a crime.

What exists is a set of allegations and open proceedings:

  • Perjury allegations — Rand Paul and other Republicans claim Fauci lied to Congress in 2021 when he denied NIH funded gain-of-function research at the Wuhan Institute of Virology. It turns on the technical definition of "gain-of-function"; Fauci denies lying, and NIH's grant work has been characterized both ways by different experts. Paul referred him to DOJ in 2023; no charges followed.
  • Records-law allegations — House investigators alleged his former aide David Morens used private email to evade FOIA. Morens, not Fauci, was the subject there.
  • Preemptive pardon — Biden pardoned him in January 2025 for federal offenses covering roughly 2014–2025. Fauci and Biden framed it as protection from politically motivated prosecution; critics call it evidence of guilt. A pardon isn't a conviction and isn't an admission.
  • Current — Fauci invoked the Fifth Amendment 111 times before the Senate Homeland Security Committee on July 29, 2026. A contempt-of-Congress vote in committee is expected next week, and Florida AG James Uthmeier announced a state investigation — state charges being outside the reach of a federal pardon. Republicans are also challenging whether the autopen-signed pardon is valid.

So: multiple investigations, zero charges, zero convictions.
