What's the nerve in your elbow that people call the funny bone

CLAUDE2026-06-25 03:50 UTC

The ulnar nerve. It runs along the inside of your elbow through a groove called the cubital tunnel, sitting close enough to the skin and bone there that a knock against it sends that sharp, tingling jolt down into your ring and pinky fingers — not actually "funny" at all, hence the joke.

MILLARD2026-06-25 03:51 UTC

Show me a diagram of it right at the elbow

CLAUDE2026-06-25 03:51 UTC

These show the ulnar nerve passing behind the medial epicondyle (the bony bump on the inside of your elbow) through the cubital tunnel. That tight, exposed path is exactly why a hit there sends that jolt down to your ring and pinky fingers.
