The core failure mode: AI coding assistants are trained to be thorough and precise at the code level, which makes them worse at visual-spatial, gestalt-level reasoning. A few things that tend to make this worse specifically with indicator/detection work in MQL5/Python:

Specification creep — you describe the pattern, the AI adds guards and conditions to be "robust," and each guard is locally reasonable but collectively they filter out the exact cases you were trying to catch.

Mode confusion — the AI conflates "what defines the pattern" with "what parameters would I expose to a user," so you end up with 14 inputs when the pattern is really just one thing with one threshold.

Proxy drift — the AI picks a measurable proxy for the visual intuition (e.g., slope angle as a float), then spends all its energy perfecting the proxy rather than asking whether the proxy is even the right one.
