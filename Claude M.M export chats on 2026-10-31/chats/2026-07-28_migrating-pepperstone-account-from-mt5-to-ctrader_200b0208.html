Migrating Pepperstone account from MT5 to cTrader

MILLARD2026-07-28 19:43 UTC

i have a pepperstone account and use mt5, i want to switch my account and money over to ctrader which pepperstone offers. how can i do that? check and be thorough to make sure

CLAUDE2026-07-28 19:43 UTC

Can't convert the MT5 account itself — you open a new cTrader account and move the money to it.

Steps:
1. Log into Secure Client Area (myaccount.pepperstone.com).
2. "Trading Accounts" → "+Add a trading account" → choose Live → select cTrader as platform. Set currency now — once a trading account is created its currency can't be changed.
3. You'll get separate cTrader login credentials (different from MT5) sent by email.
4. Internal transfer: from Client Area, transfer funds from MT5 account to the new cTrader account (no withdrawal/redeposit needed, same client entity).
5. Close out or leave the MT5 account empty — each trading account is linked to one specific platform; an MT5 account can only connect to MT5, it can't be relinked to cTrader.
6. Download cTrader desktop (Windows only) or use web/mobile, log in with new cTrader ID.

Note: cTrader commission is based on the currency you're trading, not account deposit currency — Razor cTrader commission is 7 units of the first quoted currency. For XAUUSD specifically, commission is $7 USD round-turn per lot on cTrader vs the MT4/5 lot-based schedule — check this against your current MT5 commission before moving, it changes your cost per trade.

Your EAs won't carry over — cTrader uses cAlgo/C#, not MQL5. That's a separate rewrite, not a switch.
