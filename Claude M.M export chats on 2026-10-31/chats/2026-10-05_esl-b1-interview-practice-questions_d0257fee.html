"Interview practice" could mean a job interview or a get-to-know-you speaking interview, so here are both.

Job interview (B1)
1. Can you tell me a little about yourself?
2. Why do you want this job?
3. What did you do in your last job?
4. What are your strengths?
5. What is one weakness you are working on?
6. Tell me about a problem you had at work and how you solved it.
7. Do you prefer working alone or on a team? Why?
8. How do you handle stress or pressure?
9. Where do you see yourself in five years?
10. Why should we hire you?
11. What would your last boss say about you?
12. Do you have any questions for us?

Personal / speaking interview (B1)
1. Where are you from, and what is it like there?
2. What do you do in your free time?
3. What is the best trip you have ever taken?
4. What was your favorite subject in school, and why?
5. What is something you are proud of?
6. How has your city changed in the last ten years?
7. What would you do if you won a lot of money?
8. Who is a person you admire, and why?
9. What is a skill you would like to learn?
10. What do you usually do on a typical weekend?
11. Have you ever had a funny or embarrassing experience? What happened?
12. What are your plans for the future?
