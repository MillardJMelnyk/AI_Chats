Articulate the false premises

CLAUDE2026-10-20 15:04 UTC
  1. Population grew: false; the PCBS estimated a 6% drop in the first 15 months.
  2. Genocide requires net population decline: false; the legal test is acts done with intent to destroy a group in whole or in part, so a population can grow during one, as Bosnia's did around Srebrenica.
  3. Gazans got fatter: false; child malnutrition in Gaza City tripled between May and July 2025.
  4. Starvation means everyone is emaciated: false; famine is a population threshold (20% of households in extreme food shortage, 30% of children acutely malnourished, plus set death rates), so well-fed individuals in photos don't refute it.
