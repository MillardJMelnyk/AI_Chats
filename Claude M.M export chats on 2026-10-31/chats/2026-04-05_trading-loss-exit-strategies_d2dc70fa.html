Bounce vs. Miss: bounce_pct (default 0.12). At each pivot, the script computes thresh = bounce_pct * kc_w where kc_w is KC upper minus KC lower at that bar. If the pivot price is within thresh of any KC line, it's a bounce off that line. Otherwise it's a miss, tagged to whichever KC line is nearest.

Hug vs. Parallel: hug_pct (default 0.08). Once a parallel run is detected, the script computes the average absolute spread between PT and the tracked KC line over the run. If that average spread is ≤ hug_pct * avg_kc_width, it's a hug. Otherwise it's a parallel.

So with the same 10-point KC width, a hug requires average spread ≤ 0.8 points from the line, while a parallel just needs stable spread (low delta bar-to-bar) regardless of distance.
