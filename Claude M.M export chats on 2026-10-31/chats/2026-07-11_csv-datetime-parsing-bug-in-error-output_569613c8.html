CLAUDE2026-07-11 17:47 UTC

The scanner is parsing the wrong text as TickTimeMS. Proof is in the timestamp itself: 1970.01.01 00:00:02 = 2 seconds after epoch = 2026/1000. So StringToInteger(fields[3]) is returning 2026 — meaning fields[3] begins with the date text "2026.07.10 …", not the millisecond value. Every row yields the same 2, which is why first and last are identical.

Hypothesis on the cause (the FileOpen for g_csvHandle lives in an include you didn't upload, so I can't prove which): the handle is opened in text/line mode (or with a non-comma delimiter), so each FileReadString returns an entire line rather than one field. The header skip then consumes 15 whole lines, and fields[3] is the 4th data line — which starts with the datetime string. To confirm, check the FileOpen flags in the CsvTickFeed init include: if FILE_CSV with ',' delimiter is absent, that's it.
