The bill adds up. The 2 m³ is your actual May usage, and most of what you pay is a flat fee that doesn't depend on usage.

  • Cuota base agua, $181.50: Fixed monthly charge you pay even at zero use. This is 70% of the bill.
  • Consumo de agua, $30.60: Your 2 m³ (847 − 845) at about $15.30 per m³.
  • Saneamiento, $25.45, and alcantarillado, $21.21: Water treatment and sewer fees.
  • Total: $181.50 + $30.60 + $25.45 + $21.21 = $258.76, which matches.

Consumo promedio 8: This is highly likely your unit's average monthly use over earlier billing periods, probably including months before you moved in or when more people lived there. It is shown for reference only and isn't billed. Your earlier bills or the condo administration's reading history would show how they calculate it.

Meses de adeudo 1: This only means May is unpaid, not that you're behind.
