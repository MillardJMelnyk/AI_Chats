Information Does Not Exist Without Consciousness — A Fundamental Truth Most Science Ignores

Let’s simplify the biggest misunderstood concept in science:

Recording data, measuring results, low-entropy storage, macro observation…

They are all the exact same thing.

People treat “information” like it is an objective property of the universe.

It is not.

Shannon, the father of information theory, defined it clearly:

Information is anything that eliminates uncertainty.

But here is the question almost no one asks:

Uncertainty for whom?

Uncertainty cannot exist in a dead, unconscious universe.

A particle does not feel uncertain.
A photon does not wonder.
A rock does not confuse order with chaos.

Uncertainty only exists inside a conscious observer.

Which means:

Information only exists relative to consciousness.

Without a mind to interpret it…
there is no difference between carefully recorded data and completely random noise.

Zero difference.

Think about a hard drive.

Inside it is nothing but tiny particles arranged in different physical positions.

Physically speaking:
Order and randomness are identical.

They are just matter rearranged.

The universe itself does not recognize “valid information.”
It does not recognize “records.”
It does not recognize “truth” or “pattern.”

Only consciousness can turn physical particle arrangements into meaningful information.

We constantly say:
“We recorded information.”
“We measured results.”
“We stored data.”

But every single one of these statements secretly assumes one thing:

Consciousness is present to give the universe meaning.

If all conscious life vanished from the cosmos tonight…

There would be no such thing as information.

No data.
No records.
No knowledge.

Only pure, indifferent physical chaos — equal in every way to pure, ordered “information.”

Science loves to pretend the universe runs purely on physics and mathematics.

But physics alone cannot produce information.

Information is the bridge between matter and mind.

The universe without observers is just blind particles moving blindly.

The moment consciousness disappears…
information disappears with it.

This is not philosophy.
This is pure logical deduction from the definition of information itself.

💬 Mind-blowing question below:
If information only exists because we perceive it…
does the entire structured, ordered universe only exist because of consciousness?
