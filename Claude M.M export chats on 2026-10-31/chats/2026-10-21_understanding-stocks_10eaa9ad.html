The Basilica of Our Lady of Guadalupe sits at the foot of Tepeyac Hill in northern Mexico City and is the most-visited Catholic shrine in the world, holding Juan Diego's cloak with the image of the Virgin from the 1531 apparition. The modern round basilica (1976) stands next to the old tilting 1709 one, and the big pilgrimage peaks around December 12.
