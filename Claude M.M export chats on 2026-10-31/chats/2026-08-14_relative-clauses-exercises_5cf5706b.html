B2 Relative Clauses — Practice

1. Fill in the gap (who / which / whose / where / when)
1. The manager ___ interviewed me was very professional.
2. That's the restaurant ___ we had our first date.
3. I don't remember the year ___ the company was founded.
4. She's the woman ___ car got stolen last week.
5. The report, ___ took three weeks to write, was rejected.

2. Defining vs. non-defining — add commas where needed
1. My brother who lives in Canada is a doctor. (I have only one brother)
2. My brother who lives in Canada is a doctor. (I have several brothers)
3. Paris which is the capital of France attracts millions of tourists.
4. Students who arrive late will not be admitted.

3. Combine the two sentences using a relative clause
1. The email was urgent. I sent it yesterday.
2. Mr. Torres is my old boss. He gave a speech today.
3. The hotel was fully booked. We wanted to stay there.
4. The laptop broke. I bought it last month.

4. Reduce the relative clause (omit pronoun/verb where possible)
1. The man who is standing by the door is my uncle.
2. The book which was written by Orwell is a classic.
3. Anyone who wants to leave early should tell me now.

5. Prepositions + relative pronouns
1. The company ___ I work ___ is based in Madrid. (for / which)
2. This is the tool ___ I rely ___ every day. (on / which)
3. She's the client ___ we discussed the contract ___. (with / whom)
