  2. Press Ctrl+H, leave Find what and Replace with empty, click More >>.

MILLARD2026-10-18 19:57 UTC

CLAUDE2026-10-18 19:57 UTC

No, Word's wildcard search matches characters, not fonts, and can't express "not Calibri." You can still end up with only the non-Calibri text highlighted:

  1. Press Ctrl+A, then click Text Highlight Color on the Home tab to highlight everything.
  2. Press Ctrl+H, leave both boxes empty, click More >>.
  3. Click in Find what, then Format > Font..., pick Calibri, OK.
  4. Click in Replace with, then Format > Highlight twice, so the line under the box reads "Not Highlight."
  5. Click Replace All: only the text that isn't Calibri stays highlighted.
