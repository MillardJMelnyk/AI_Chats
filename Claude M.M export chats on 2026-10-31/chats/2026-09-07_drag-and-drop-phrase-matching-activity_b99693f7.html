Can you also make me an exercise along with pictures whwere there is a paragraph or 2 and with blanks where prepositions should be and have the blanks so the student can write in the correct preposition

CLAUDE2026-09-08 00:44 UTC

Twenty gaps in one story, in two parts.

Part 1 is movement prepositions (out of, towards, across, under, up, down, through, past, into, away from), with six pictures above the text: the garage, the stone bridge, the railway bridge, the hill, the tunnel, the cinema.

Part 2 is place prepositions (at, next to, opposite, between, behind, in front of, near, on, under, in), with a street map showing the market, bank, café, post office, cinema and car park, so she can work out each answer from the picture.

Each word is used once, and it greys out in the word bank as she types it. Check answers marks green or red, Show answers fills them in, and it prints cleanly if you want it on paper.
