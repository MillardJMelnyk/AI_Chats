My son defined ignorance as being the result of ignoring information that you could have been familiar with.

CLAUDE2026-09-06 04:59 UTC

That's willful ignorance specifically — plain ignorance is just not knowing, whether or not the information was ever within reach. His version does land on the real root though: ignorare means both "not know" and "disregard," so ignore and ignorance are the same word.

MILLARD2026-09-06 05:00 UTC

Yeah it seems more logical to think of it that way.

What's the difference between willful ignorance and motivated ignorance?

CLAUDE2026-09-06 05:00 UTC

Willful ignorance is deliberate avoidance of information you know exists — you choose not to look. Motivated ignorance is avoidance driven by what you want to be true; the wanting steers you away, often without you noticing you're steering.
