Banner entries — Mandate era (pre-state militias)
- Jerusalem 1937: Irgun's "Black Sunday," Nov 14, 10 Arabs killed and many more wounded. HL
- Haifa 1937: No Haifa massacre found for 1937; that year's recorded Irgun killings were in Jerusalem and 2 Arabs killed on Bat Yam beach. U as dated.
- Haifa 1938: Irgun market bombs: on 6 July 1938, 21 Arabs were killed and 52 wounded; on 25 July a second market bomb killed at least 39 Arabs and injured 70. HL
- Balad al-Sheikh 1939: A small Haganah reprisal killing ~5 is reported in some accounts, but this search didn't confirm it; the banner may be confusing it with the far larger 1947 massacre (below). U as listed.
- Haifa 1939: Feb 27: 33 Arabs killed in multiple attacks, including 24 by a bomb in Haifa's Suk Quarter; June 19: 20 killed by explosives on a donkey at a Haifa market. HL
- Haifa 1947: Dec 30, Irgun militants threw two bombs from a car into a crowd of Arab workers, killing 6 and wounding 42. Arab coworkers then killed 39 Jewish workers; the Haganah's reprisal at Balad al-Sheikh killed between 60 and 70, including women and children. HL
- Abbasiya 1947: Irgun attack Dec 13; one contemporaneous tally says 12 Arabs killed with mortars and automatic weapons. L
- Al-Khisas 1947: Dec 18 Palmach raid, 10 Arabs killed and 5 wounded. HL
- Bab al-Amud (Damascus Gate) 1947: Irgun bombs Dec 13 and Dec 29; the Dec 29 attack killed 15 Arabs. HL
- Jerusalem 1947: Probably a duplicate of Damascus Gate; no separate event pinned. U as distinct.
- Sheikh Bureik 1947: No record found. U
- Jaffa 1948: Jan 4 Lehi (not Irgun) truck bomb at the Saraya, roughly 15–26 killed. L

Banner entries — Israeli state forces or settlers
- Khan Yunis 1956: UNRWA reported Israeli forces killed 275 Palestinians: 140 refugees and 135 local residents. HL
- Jerusalem 1967: No discrete massacre identifiable. U as a named event.
- Sabra & Shatila 1982: Phalangist militia did the killing inside IDF-sealed camps; Kahan Commission used IDF intelligence's 700–800, Palestinian researchers named ~1,300+, Palestinian Red Crescent said 2,000+. Kahan found Sharon personally responsible. HL event, L range.
- Al-Aqsa 1990: Some 17 to 21 Palestinians killed and more than 150 wounded by Israeli security forces. HL
- Ibrahimi Mosque 1994: Goldstein killed 29 Muslims praying inside, wounding 125 more. HL
- Jenin 2002: 52 Palestinians killed (HRW, UN), at least 22 civilians; 23 IDF soldiers killed. HRW rejected "massacre" but documented unlawful killings. HL
- Gaza 2008–09: B'Tselem 1,391 killed (759 not fighting, 318 minors); 13 Israelis. HL
- Gaza 2012: B'Tselem 167 (87 not fighting); 6 Israelis. L
- Gaza 2014: UN OCHA 2,251 (1,462 civilians, 551 children); 73 Israelis, 67 of them soldiers. HL
- Gaza 2018–19: Great March of Return, ~214 protesters killed (OCHA); 1 Israeli soldier. L
- Gaza 2021: OCHA 261 (67 children); 13 killed in Israel. HL
- Gaza 2023–ongoing: Health Ministry count 74,237 dead and 175,206 injured as of Oct 4, 2026; 1,454 killed since the ceasefire began Oct 11, 2025. That includes at least 21,000 children. A Lancet study put traumatic deaths ~41% above the ministry count through mid-2024. Genocide finding: UN Commission of Inquiry, Amnesty, IAGS, B'Tselem say yes; ICJ case pending; Israel denies. The Oct 7 attack killed ~1,200 in Israel (Israeli count). HL

Major omissions
- Deir Yassin, Apr 1948: Irgun/Lehi, ~107–110. HL
- Lydda, Jul 1948: IDF, ~250 by IDF reports, 400+ by Palestinian estimates; 50–70k expelled. HL
- Tantura, May 1948: ~200 per veterans' testimony, disputed. L
- Dawayima, Oct 1948: 80–100 per IDF soldier testimony; village mukhtar said 455. HL event, L count
- Qibya, 1953: Unit 101 under Sharon, 69 killed, mostly women and children. HL
- Kafr Qasim, 1956: Border Police, 48 (49 counting an unborn child). HL
- Rafah, 1956: UNRWA said Israeli forces killed 110 Palestinians in Rafah after the fighting was over. HL
- Land Day 1976: 6. October 2000: 13 Palestinian citizens of Israel. HL
- 2024–25 Gaza: Flour massacre, 112+ killed waiting for aid; over 2,500 killed approaching GHF aid sites, May–Oct 2025. HL

Mandate-era context: from September 1937 through 1939, 5,000 Arabs, 415 Jews and several hundred Britons were killed, with most Arab deaths at British hands, so several 1937–47 entries were part of tit-for-tat cycles rather than one-sided massacres.
