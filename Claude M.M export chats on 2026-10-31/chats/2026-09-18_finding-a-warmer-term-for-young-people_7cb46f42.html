what's a friendly, familiar, on-your-side substitute for "young people"? That expression sounds clinical, like I'm watching them in their fishbowl.

CLAUDE2026-09-18 16:04 UTC

"Young folks," or, if you're speaking to them directly, "you all."

MILLARD2026-09-18 16:05 UTC

Obviously, I'm referring to them 3rd person. "Folks" does belong to boomers like me, but I wouldn't use it except in the most general sense. Try some more.

CLAUDE2026-09-18 16:05 UTC
  • "The kids" (affectionate, if they won't read it as talking down)
  • "Our kids" (puts you on their side)
  • "The younger crowd"
  • "The next generation"
  • "The ones coming up behind us"
  • "Younger people" (plainer, but less fishbowl than "young people")
  • "Twenty-somethings" (only if the age range fits)
