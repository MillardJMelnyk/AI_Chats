1 example from each tense

CLAUDE2026-08-20 17:51 UTC

Present: Carezco de paciencia. — I lack patience.
Imperfect: La casa carecía de agua. — The house lacked water.
Preterite: El plan careció de apoyo. — The plan lacked support.
Future: Careceremos de tiempo. — We'll lack time.
Conditional: Carecerías de razón. — You would lack reason.
Present subj.: Dudo que carezcan de fondos. — I doubt they lack funds.
Imperfect subj.: Si careciera de dinero, te avisaría. — If I lacked money, I'd tell you.

MILLARD2026-08-20 17:52 UTC

difference between imperfect and preterite

CLAUDE2026-08-20 17:52 UTC

Preterite = a completed action, viewed as a finished event with a start and end.
Imperfect = an ongoing, habitual, or background state, with no defined end.

Carecía de agua — it lacked water (that was the ongoing situation).
Careció de agua — it lacked water (during a specific, closed period, now over).
