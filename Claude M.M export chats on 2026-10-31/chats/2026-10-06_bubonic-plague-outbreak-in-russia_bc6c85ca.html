What's this I'm hearing about bubonic plague in Russia

CLAUDE2026-10-06 12:30 UTC

Tuesday, October 6, 2026, 6:30 AM CST

It's pneumonic plague, not bubonic, and it's one suspected lab death in Siberia, not an outbreak. On October 1 the inpatient unit at the district hospital in Shelekhov, Irkutsk region, closed over a suspected "especially dangerous infection," and media later reported that 28-year-old Darya Shipilova had died there of pneumonic plague. One account says she was a lab technician at the Irkutsk Anti-Plague Institute who broke a test tube containing the bacterium, though local authorities say there's no evidence supporting the test-tube story.

Official status is murky. Rospotrebnadzor calls it "pneumonia of unknown aetiology" and says Irkutsk and Shelekhov are "stable", while Buryatia's head, Alexei Tsydenov, said she died from an unspecified form of plague. 189 people are under observation, and the State Department says it's closely monitoring.

Risk read: Highly unlikely to become a wider epidemic. An LSHTM expert says a lab exposure to one person isn't an outbreak and seems unlikely to spread widely, and plague is bacterial and generally treatable with antibiotics if caught quickly. Missing evidence: lab confirmation of plague and whether any of the 189 contacts get sick.
