The bill mentions two electronic/digital payment options:

  1. CFE-contigo app — The front of the bill prominently advertises this as the "authorized app." You can download it and pay through it directly.

  2. cfemx portal — The back mentions checking cfemx.mx under "medios de pago" (payment methods).

  • In the CFE-contigo app, make sure your card is registered as a débito (debit) card, not credit — some Mexican payment processors require you to select the type explicitly.
  • Some debit cards require you to enable compras en línea (online purchases) through your bank's app before they'll work on third-party platforms.
  • The physical store options on the back (OXXO, 7-Eleven, Chedraui, Soriana, etc.) accept cash and are the most reliable fallback if the digital route keeps failing.

There is no CLABE number printed on either page of this bill. CFE may offer SPEI payment but they don't advertise it here.

Your best bet to find CFE's CLABE is:

  • Check cfe.mx directly — look under "formas de pago" or "pago por transferencia"
  • Call CFE's customer line 071 and ask specifically for their CLABE for SPEI payment

Be aware that even if you find a CLABE, you'd also need your service number (079191104019) or account number in the payment reference field so CFE can match the payment to your account. Confirm that format when you call.

The most practical path given you already have Wise:

Mercado Pago (Mercado Libre's payment app) is worth looking at first because:

For an actual Mexican debit card, most options require a CURP (Mexican ID number), which you won't have until residency is established. Once you have even temporary residency:
- Nu Mexico (Nubank) — fully digital, fast approval
- Hey Banco — similar
- Klar or Stori — fintechs with simpler onboarding

For right now, the Mercado Pago route is your best shot without residency documents. I'd verify their current signup requirements since they do change, but historically they've been more accessible to people without full Mexican documentation.
