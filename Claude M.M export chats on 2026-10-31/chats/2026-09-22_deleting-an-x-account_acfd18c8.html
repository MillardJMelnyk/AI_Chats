how to delete x account

CLAUDE2026-09-22 00:17 UTC

On x.com: More → Settings and Privacy → Your account → Deactivate your account → Deactivate → enter password and confirm. If you don't log in for 30 days after that, the account is permanently deleted.

how to delete on android app?

CLAUDE2026-09-22 00:17 UTC

Tap your profile photo (top left) → Settings & Support → Settings and privacy → Your account → Deactivate your account → Deactivate → enter your password and confirm. On Android, you open the Settings & Support tab first and then tap Settings and privacy; the account is deleted if you don't log in for 30 days.
