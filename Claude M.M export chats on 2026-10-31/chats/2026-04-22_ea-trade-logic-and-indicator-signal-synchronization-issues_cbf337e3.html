Here's the proposal.

Current state: eff_sens is the single knob controlling how easily a reversal fires. It's either trigger_sens or 0.0 (reverted mode). Blackout period (eff_blackout) is the other lever. Neither accounts for spread direction, KC zone, or KC direction.

Proposal — three multipliers applied to eff_sens:

  1. Spread-alignment multiplier. Compare signal direction to the MA1-MA2 spread sign. Signal with the spread (e.g. long when MA1 > MA2) = normal sensitivity. Signal against the spread = reduced eff_sens (easier to fire). One input parameter: InpAgainstSpreadSensMult (e.g. 0.5 = half the usual sensitivity threshold when going against the spread).

  2. KC zone multiplier. Four zones: above upper band, between upper and middle, between middle and lower, below lower band. A long signal from below the lower band is deep against — make it easier. A long signal from above the upper band is extended — make it harder.   3. KC direction multiplier. KC_dir derived from KC middle slope (you already compute kc_mid_slope). Signal with KC_dir = normal. Signal against KC_dir = reduced eff_sens. One input parameter: InpAgainstKcDirSensMult.
