I'm Canadian, have an active green card lived continuously in the United States since 1964, and I've been on Social Security for 10 years. Do I have anything to worry about from this "cleanup"?

CLAUDE2026-10-21 16:59 UTC

Wednesday, October 21, 2026, 10:59 AM CST

Unlikely: green card holders can collect Social Security, and SSA treats them the same as citizens for retirement, disability, and survivor benefits, so the cleanup should just tag your record as a lawful permanent resident. The only real risk is a data error, such as a wrong death date (17 million were added) or a mismatch, because a 2006 SSA audit estimated nearly 18 million records with citizenship listed had wrong information.

MILLARD2026-10-21 16:59 UTC

risk is negligible then?

CLAUDE2026-10-21 16:59 UTC

Yes for the odds, highly likely: you're an active beneficiary with a 60-year record, which is the best-maintained kind of file SSA has. The impact is not negligible if an error does hit, because a wrong death date stops payments until you prove you're alive.
