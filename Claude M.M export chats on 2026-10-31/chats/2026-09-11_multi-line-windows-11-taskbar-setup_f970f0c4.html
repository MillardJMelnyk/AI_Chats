MILLARD2026-09-11 21:15 UTC

how can I make the win 11 taskbar multiple lines?

CLAUDE2026-09-11 21:15 UTC

Not possible natively — you need a third-party tool: Windhawk (install the "Multirow taskbar for Windows 11" mod, free), ExplorerPatcher (free), or Start11 (paid).

MILLARD2026-09-11 21:16 UTC

what does the native taskbar do when you've got more than one line's worth of icons?

CLAUDE2026-09-11 21:16 UTC

It scrolls — the icons stay one row and you get small arrow chevrons at the ends to page through the overflow (and with labels on, the labels truncate first, then buttons combine if you've set that).
