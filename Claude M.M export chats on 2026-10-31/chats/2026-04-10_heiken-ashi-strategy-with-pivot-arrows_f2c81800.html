I'll read the file first, then give you my honest assessment.

Alright, I see what Codex did and why it's not producing much.

Yes, I understand HA well enough. Here's the core concept:

Heiken Ashi smooths price by averaging. The key signals aren't the candle colors themselves but the body characteristics:
- Strong trend up: No lower wick (or tiny), full body
- Strong trend down: No upper wick (or tiny), full body
- Indecision/reversal zone: Small body, wicks both sides (spinning top / doji-like)
- Color flip: Often a lagging confirmation, not a leading signal

Why this code produces few arrows:

Codex is requiring two independent conditions simultaneously:
1. An HA color flip (bearish-to-bullish or vice versa)
2. A classic left/right pivot structure on raw price

That's extremely restrictive. HA flips don't align well with strict swing pivots because HA is smoothed — it lags. By the time HA flips color, the raw price pivot may have already passed (outside the right-bars window), or the pivot structure doesn't meet the strict "lower than all neighbors" test.

What you probably want instead — options:

  1. HA-based PPM pivots, not raw-price pivots. Run the pivot logic on the HA values themselves (HA low/high buffers), not g_rates[].low/high. This keeps everything in HA-space where the flip actually happens.

  2. HA body-state transitions, not just color flips. Track when HA transitions from "no lower wick" (strong up) to "wick appears" (momentum loss). That's more predictive than waiting for the full color flip.

  3. Drop the strict pivot requirement. Instead, mark the first HA flip after N consecutive same-color bars. That's closer to what traders actually watch — a trend break, not a geometric swing point.

  4. Combine HA with something directional. HA alone is a smoothing lens. Pairing it with a momentum read (slope of HA close, or even your TEMA work) would give you "HA flip + momentum divergence" which is more meaningful than "HA flip + raw pivot."

My recommendation:

If your goal is "PPM pivots" — predominant price movement reversals — I'd do option 1 (pivots on HA values) combined with a minimum-bars-in-trend requirement before a flip counts. That filters out the chop and only marks flips that end a sustained HA run.
