HOW TO USE JETPACK IN MUCK GAME?

CLAUDE2026-10-10 03:19 UTC

Friday, October 9, 2026, 9:19 PM CST — you don't activate it: once you pick up a Jetpack it automatically raises your jump height (it doesn't let you fly), and each extra one you collect stacks for higher jumps. Get them from coin-opened loot chests, mainly the Black and White ones since it's a common powerup.

how to make bow?

CLAUDE2026-10-10 03:39 UTC

Build a Fletching Table at your Workbench with 25 Birch Wood and 10 Flint, then turn a few wood into bark, the bark into rope, and the bow will show up in the Fletching Table menu (bows only craft there, not at the Workbench).

what is ancient core for?

CLAUDE2026-10-10 03:57 UTC

It's used to repair the shipwreck's engine to reach the game's ending; you only need one, and it has a decent chance to drop from Gronk or Big Chunk. The Ancient Core slot won't show until you've built the Ship Engine.
