Prunes vs prune juice for constipation

Created 2026-09-28 23:58 UTC · Last updated 2026-09-28 23:58 UTC · 2 messages
Original: claude.ai/chat/d6ed385e-6a0d-4b28-af65-f121fbc39684
MILLARD2026-09-28 23:58 UTC

Is there any difference between eating prunes and drinking prune juice when it comes to relieving constipation?

CLAUDE2026-09-28 23:58 UTC

Yes: whole prunes contain both fiber and sorbitol (a natural laxative sugar), while prune juice keeps most of the sorbitol but loses most of the fiber, so whole prunes are generally more effective and have stronger study support.
