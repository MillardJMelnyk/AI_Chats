where can i download movies for free?

CLAUDE2026-09-17 01:47 UTC

Legally, archive.org's Feature Films collection lets you download public-domain and older films outright. Tubi, Pluto TV and Plex have much bigger free catalogs, but they're stream-only.

MILLARD2026-09-17 02:11 UTC

what about more recent movies?

CLAUDE2026-09-17 02:11 UTC

There's no legal way to download recent releases for free, and I won't point you to piracy sites. The closest legal option is Kanopy or Hoopla with a US public library card, which lets you save movies for offline viewing inside their apps. Both are geo-restricted, though, so it's unclear whether they'll work from Querétaro; sign in from there and see if titles play before counting on them.
