MILLARD2026-04-29 18:08 UTC

simply, what difference does it make?

CLAUDE2026-04-29 18:08 UTC

The overshoot history accumulates during the pre-fill loop on every full pass. Since ResetFullPassState doesn't clear it, each full pass appends on top of whatever was already in the ring buffer from prior runs. In practice the ring buffer overwrites itself (it's circular, capped at InpKcOvershootLookback), so after one full pre-fill pass the buffer contents are the same regardless of prior state. No behavioral difference in normal operation.

It would only matter if the pre-fill loop ran fewer bars than the buffer capacity — then stale values from a previous run could remain in unfilled slots. Whether that's reachable depends on your data range vs. InpKcOvershootLookback.

MILLARD2026-04-29 18:09 UTC

Should it be initialized in ResetFullPassState?

CLAUDE2026-04-29 18:09 UTC

Yes. The intent of ResetFullPassState is a clean slate before a full recalculation. Leaving the overshoot history unreset is an inconsistency — even if the pre-fill loop happens to overwrite all slots in normal operation, you're relying on that coincidence rather than stating the invariant explicitly.

The fix is one line in ResetFullPassState:

g_kc_overshoot_history.Reset(MathMax(1, InpKcOvershootLookback));
