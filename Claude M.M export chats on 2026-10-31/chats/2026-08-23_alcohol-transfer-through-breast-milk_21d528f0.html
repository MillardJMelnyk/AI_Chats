How much alcohol gets through to a nursing baby

CLAUDE2026-08-23 21:16 UTC

Milk alcohol tracks blood alcohol almost exactly — same concentration — so a baby nursing at your peak gets roughly 5–6% of your weight-adjusted dose.

Practical numbers:

  • Peaks 30–60 min after a drink (60–90 if you drank with food)
  • Clears at about 2–2.5 hours per standard drink; two drinks ≈ 4–5 hours
  • Pumping and dumping does nothing to speed it up — it leaves milk as it leaves blood
  • One drink, waited out or nursed right before drinking, is considered low-risk by most guidelines

Regular heavy exposure is the real concern — it's linked to sedation and slower motor development, not the occasional glass.
