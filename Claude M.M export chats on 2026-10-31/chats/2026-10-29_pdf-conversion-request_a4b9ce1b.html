MILLARD2026-10-29 17:12 UTC

on ctrader, how do i do a trailing stop of 5 pips so that after my trad appreciate to 5 pips it follows it form there like it does in mt5?

CLAUDE2026-10-29 17:12 UTC

cTrader's built-in trailing stop has no "start after X profit" setting — it trails at the size of your regular stop loss from the moment you set it — so for MT5-style behavior you need a cBot like EarnForex's free Trailing Stop on Profit, where the stop-loss follows the price gradually but only once a certain level of profit is attained. Check your broker's gold pip size in cTrader's symbol info first, since on many cTrader feeds 5 pips on XAUUSD is only a $0.05 move.

MILLARD2026-10-29 17:21 UTC

so if i put it at 5 pips it will trail at 5 pips?

CLAUDE2026-10-29 17:21 UTC

Yes — the stop stays 5 pips behind the best price, but it starts trailing immediately from entry (stop begins at −5 pips), not after you're 5 pips in profit like MT5.
