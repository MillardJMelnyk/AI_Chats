MILLARD2026-05-13 22:09 UTC

Overall: good direction, but I see two logic issues.
First issue: retests are overcounted. The current retest count includes the first time price enters the near-extreme band on the way into the anchor. That is not really a retest. A retest should mean: price was near the extreme, left that area, then came back. So the first entry should not count.
Second issue: loiter time is undercounted. The current loiter logic only counts time when two neighboring ticks are both inside the near-extreme band. If one tick enters the band and the next tick leaves, it counts zero time, even though price was inside the band between those ticks. For tick data, the cleaner interpretation is: if the current tick is inside the band, count the time until the next tick.
Other than that, the structure is solid.
I like that Phase 2 now covers approach, recovery, and full-window behavior. I also like that Phase 3 clusters on shape and keeps raw values for interpretation.
One caution: the recovery-speed ratio can explode when approach speed is near zero. That may create a junk cluster unless capped or guarded with a small floor.
