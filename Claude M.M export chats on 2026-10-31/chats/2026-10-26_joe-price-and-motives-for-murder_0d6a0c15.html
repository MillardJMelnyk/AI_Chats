CLAUDE2026-10-26 13:17 UTC

No source I can reach states Cubbler's actual motive; the theories circulating hang on threads in the NYT's reporting of the NCIS file, each Unlikely on current evidence:

  1. Afghan girl's death. An explosion on Dec. 16 killed a little girl at a site near an outpost Price oversaw that had been used for mortar firing, though a U.S. report said no unexploded ordnance had been left there. That report was folded in his trouser pocket when he was found. The theory is that he wasn't going to sign off on it.
  2. Detainee killing at Kalach. Price was present when Cmdr. Mike Hayes called in Navy investigators over Army soldiers' accusations that three SEALs had abused detainees at Kalach, leading to one Afghan's death. The SEALs' commander later used his authority to drop the charges.
  3. Drugs in his own command group. A later Group 2 commander held an all-hands meeting over a drug problem in Group 2, and SEALs told CBS that blowing the whistle on drug use gets you blackballed or driven out. This is the likeliest fit for Tucker's "criminal cartel" framing.
  4. Resentment from his own men. After Ebbert's death, SEALs complained it was the worst deployment and leadership they had seen.
