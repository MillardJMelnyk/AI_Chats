CLAUDE2026-09-12 01:52 UTC

Firefox → Settings → General → scroll to "Digital Rights Management (DRM) Content" → check "Play DRM-controlled content". Then about:addons → Plugins → make sure "Widevine Content Decryption Module" is set to Always Activate and updated.

If it's already on: close Firefox, delete the gmp-widevinecdm folder in your profile directory, restart, and reload the page to force a fresh download. Private Browsing windows and Enhanced Tracking Protection set to Strict also block it.
