Where can I read about Jewish mythology such as Abraham and the idols?
Claude2025-12-23 13:22 UTC

Primary Sources:

  1. Genesis Rabbah (Bereshit Rabbah) - Midrashic commentary on Genesis, sections 38:13 and surrounding passages contain the story of Abraham destroying Terah's idols.

  2. Tanchuma - Another midrashic collection with versions of this narrative.

  3. Sefer HaYashar (Book of Jasher) - Medieval text that elaborates the Abraham-idol stories extensively.

English Translations:

  • Midrash Rabbah: Genesis, translated by H. Freedman (Soncino Press) - widely available
  • Online: Sefaria.org has searchable English translations of Genesis Rabbah and other midrashic texts

Note: These stories are not in the Hebrew Bible (Torah/Tanakh) itself. They are post-biblical elaborations from rabbinic tradition, dated roughly 4th-6th century CE for Genesis Rabbah.
