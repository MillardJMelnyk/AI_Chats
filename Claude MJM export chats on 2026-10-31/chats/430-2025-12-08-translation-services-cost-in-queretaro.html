Based on the search results (December 2025):

Remote/Telephone Interpretation (Conference Call):

  • Latin America regional rates: $80–$250 USD/hour for human interpreters
  • Salaried interpreter equivalent in Mexico: ~MXN $160/hour (~$8 USD at current exchange)
  • Agency rates for remote interpretation: Generally 20-30% less than on-site services

Key variables affecting price:

  • Type: Consecutive interpretation (speaker pauses) is cheaper than simultaneous (real-time)
  • Language pair: Spanish-English is a common, lower-cost combination
  • Duration: Most interpreters require 2-4 hour minimum booking
  • Specialization: General business interpretation costs less than legal/medical/technical

Practical estimate for Querétaro conference call interpretation:

  • Freelance/direct hire: ~MXN $800–$2,000/hour ($40–$100 USD)
  • Agency rates: ~MXN $1,600–$4,000/hour ($80–$200 USD)
  • AI-assisted platforms: ~$60 USD/hour

One certified translation company (certifiedtranslations.com.mx) explicitly lists offices in both CDMX and Querétaro, offering video remote interpretation integrated with Zoom.

What's your specific use case? Language pair, consecutive vs. simultaneous, technical subject matter, and duration would narrow the estimate.
