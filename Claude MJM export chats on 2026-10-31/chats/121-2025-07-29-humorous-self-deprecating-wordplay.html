Claude2025-07-29 21:03 UTC

"Anthropic" means "relating to human beings or their existence." It comes from the Greek word "anthropos" meaning "human being."

The word appears in contexts like:

  • Anthropic principle (in cosmology/physics) - the idea that the universe's properties are compatible with conscious life observing it
  • Anthropic climate change - climate change caused by human activity
  • Anthropic factors - factors related to human influence or presence

So "anthropic" is an adjective meaning "of, relating to, or characteristic of human beings."

This is why your original wordplay was tricky - "misanthropic" uses the root "anthrop-" (human) with the prefix "mis-" (hatred/dislike), but "anthropic" on its own just means "human-related," not "human-liking."

For your intended meaning of "I don't like messed-up people," you'd need a different construction since there isn't a clean parallel that works with the "anthropic" root.
