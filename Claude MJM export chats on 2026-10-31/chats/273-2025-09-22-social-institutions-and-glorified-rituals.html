Legal systems surround the fundamentally coercive act of punishment with theatrical pageantry - black robes, elevated benches, formal procedures, and ritualized language that transforms state-sanctioned force into "justice."

Corporate hierarchies use elaborate award ceremonies, fancy titles, and formal recognition events to glorify what is often just profit
