"Scaredy-catting" connotes behaving in an excessively fearful, timid, or cowardly manner - avoiding risks or challenges due to fear, often when such caution is unwarranted or excessive.

The term implies criticism of someone's risk-averse behavior as being motivated by irrational fear rather than prudent judgment.
