Compilation of Claims About DARPA Involvement in mRNA Vaccines and COVID Response

SASHA LATYPOVA (former pharma executive, analyst):

  1. mRNA technology and bioweapons circumvention: mRNA technology "gets around the Bioweapons Convention restrictions because it is a synthetic chemical drug, not a live pathogen," while live pathogens are banned. A carve-out in the treaty allows "small quantities of offensive bioweapons...to be developed in order to do research on vaccine countermeasures."

  2. DOD spending and control: In 2020-2021, DOD spent approximately $50 billion funding COVID products alone—roughly 50% of U.S. pharmaceutical industry R&D spending annually. This means "there is no private bio-pharmaceutical manufacturing industry in the U.S. It is all controlled by the government and specifically, by the DOD."

  3. Col. Matt Hepburn role: Highlighted the role of Col. Matt Hepburn of DARPA as helping "mastermind the COVID-19 pandemic preparedness and response" and DOD linkages with Big Pharma "years before the pandemic."

  4. Long-term DARPA investment: DARPA has been working on genetic vaccines with Moderna since 2011 (ADEPT program started 2011), not developed "in just a few months" as Operation Warp Speed portrayed.

  5. OTA contracts and manufacturing standards: Using "Other Transactions Authority" (OTA) contracts bypassed normal safety and efficacy requirements for vaccines including Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP), allowing shots to be "unleashed at warp speed in a matter of months."

  6. Products as "prototypes" not drugs: Countermeasures classified as "prototypes" under OTA contracts, which means manufacturing is merely a "demonstration," requiring "basically no regulatory oversight."

  7. Regulatory capture: FDA "giving sham approvals" and is largely "theatre" since it's "powerless to stop" DOD operations.

  8. Corporate structure: Vaccine companies (Pfizer, Moderna, etc.) are "marketing shields for the military program." DOD is "Chief Operating Officer" while HHS has "Chief Science Advisor" role.

  9. DOD as developer: Because "the US Department of Defense, under the Emergency Use Authorization countermeasures program, is the 'developer' of the vaccines," not private companies.

  10. Advanced Technologies International (ATI): DOD/BARDA contracts run through ATI as vendor management company.

  11. China connection: DOD awarded $10 billion to ventures with "substantial equity and IP holder" being Chinese Communist Party (via Fosun Pharmaceuticals connection with Pfizer/BioNTech).

DENIS RANCOURT (physicist, former tenured professor):

  1. No pandemic, but military operation: COVID "was a multi-pronged attack by the USA regime...designed and run by the USA regime" with "prominent direct contractual and operational involvement by the USA DoD."

  2. Vaccine deaths and targeting: Quantified vaccine-caused deaths at "1 death for every 2,000 injections, increasing exponentially with age...as large as 1 death for every 100 injections for those 80 years and older." Estimated 13-17 million deaths worldwide from vaccines.

  3. DOD funds gain-of-function: "The Department of Defence (DoD) does not fund gain-of-function and bioweapon research (abroad, in particular)."

  4. Mortality patterns defy epidemiology: Synchronized mortality spikes globally that make no epidemiological sense for a spreading respiratory disease—suggesting military medical intervention rather than natural pandemic.

  5. Cationic nanoparticles toxicity: The cationic nanoparticles in mRNA injections are "particularly dangerous, they're cytotoxic to cells" and "The toxicity was well known up until 2019, and then we just forgot that these things were extremely toxic."

  6. Vaccine as bioweapon delivery system: If anything COVID is "close to being a bioweapon, it is the military capacity to massively, and repeatedly, rollout individual injections, which are physical vectors."

KATHERINE WATT (Pennsylvania paralegal, legal researcher):

  1.     • 2004: Project Bioshield Act
    • 2005: PREP Act of 2005
