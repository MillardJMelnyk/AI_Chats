No.

The statement describes situations meeting either of two conditions:

  1. Product may cause temporary/reversible adverse health consequences, OR
  2. Probability of serious adverse health consequences is remote

These conditions are disjunctive (connected by "or"), not contradictory. They describe different risk profiles:

  • Condition 1: Minor/reversible harm (probability unspecified)
  • Condition 2: Serious harm unlikely

No logical contradiction exists unless "serious adverse health consequences" is defined to include "temporary or medically reversible" consequences—which contradicts standard medical/regulatory usage where these are distinct severity categories.
