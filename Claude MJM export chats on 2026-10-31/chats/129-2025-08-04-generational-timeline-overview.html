Claude2025-08-04 15:25 UTC

Here are the generational time periods:

Baby Boomers: 1946-1964 Generation X: 1965-1980 Millennials (Gen Y): 1981-1996 Generation Z: 1997-2012 Generation Alpha: 2013-present

Note that exact boundaries vary slightly depending on the source, but these are the most commonly accepted ranges.

Millard2025-08-04 15:25 UTC
Interesting, "Alpha" because they ran out of letters?
Claude2025-08-04 15:25 UTC

Not exactly - they didn't run out of letters, but they did reach the end of the alphabet with Gen Z.

The naming actually follows a pattern: after going through X, Y, Z, researchers (particularly Mark McCrindle who popularized the term) decided to start fresh with the Greek alphabet rather than continue with something like "Gen AA."

"Alpha" suggests a new beginning - fitting since Gen Alpha is the first generation born entirely in the 21st century and is growing up fully immersed in smartphones, AI, and social media from birth. It's meant to signal they're the start of a new cycle of generational naming.

After Alpha will presumably come Beta, Gamma, etc.
