I stepped through the code carefully. Here is my assessment, broken into GPT's two claims and what GPT missed.

GPT Claim 1 — "State drift from reprocessing prev_calculated - 1"

Partially confirmed, but the mechanism described is wrong, and the actual live-only bug is different.

GPT says reprocessing bar prev_calculated - 1 on each tick accumulates drifted state because state isn't rewound. True in structure: line 735 sets start_bar = prev_calculated - 1 with no reset. But in practice, on steady-state ticks of the same forming bar, prev_calculated == rates_total, so start_bar = rates_total - 1 = the forming bar itself. Only one bar is processed. No re-processing of old bars. The drift GPT describes is substantially idempotent.

The actual live-only bug is simpler and more serious: flip_check_pending (line 1231) evaluates logical_close, which in live mode is a tick price — not the final bar close. With InpSignalBlackout = 0 and flip_check_bar = signal_bar, the condition i >= flip_check_bar is satisfied immediately on the next tick of the SAME bar where the short fired. So: the short fires at bar A, flip_check_pending = true, flip_check_bar = A. On the very next tick of bar A (price may have moved up transiently), the flip fires with force_committed = true, producing the spurious long.

In historical whole-bar mode, bar A's flip_check evaluates close[A] = the final close. If the bar ultimately closed below the threshold (market moved down), no flip. In live mode, any intra-bar tick above the threshold fires it. That is the complete explanation for why this is live-only.

GPT Claim 2 — "Chart_TryCock and Chart_ResetCycle blocked by i < logical_n - 1"

Confirmed exactly. Lines 1094, 1102, and 1386 all gate on i < logical_n - 1. The forming bar (i == rates_total - 1) cannot cock, reset cycle, or clear a qualified cross. This is real.

However, GPT presents this as a cause of being "stuck." It is a delay, not a permanent block. After the (spurious) long fires at bar A with cross_bar = A:

  • Chart_TryCock requires cand > cross_bar = A. Valid candidate cand = A+1 becomes available only when processing bar A+2 as a closed bar, which first occurs on bar A+3's opening tick.
  • The earliest a short-cock can fire is bar A+3's first tick, using close[A+2] < open[A+1].
  • The earliest a reversal signal can then fire is during bar A+3's formation if close[A+3] < ma2[A+3] - eff_sens.

In historical mode the same 3-bar minimum gap exists (using final closes). The live-specific compounding is that the 3-bar dead zone starts from the spuriously-triggered long, not from where it would have started in historical mode. By bar A+3, the bulk of the down move may be spent.

What GPT missed — the post-signal Chart_ResetCycle overwrite

Lines 1378–1384 set i_dir = signal (e.g., 1 for the forced long). Lines 1386–1396 then check qualified_cross and, if true and i < logical_n - 1, call Chart_ResetCycle which overwrites i_dir with curr_t2e. If curr_t2e = -1 (MA1 crossed below MA2 at bar A/B), i_dir becomes −1 despite the long signal.

With i_dir = -1, Chart_TryCock looks for close[bar] > open[cand] to cock long. Market is going down: close < open, no long cock. Indicator is now permanently looking for a long re-entry into a downtrend. Same-direction guard (line 1251) blocks that long even if it cocked. Result: completely stuck. This happens in both modes when it occurs, but the live-mode flip creates the context that exposes it.

Summary

GPT is right that there are two distinct bugs and right that i < logical_n - 1 blocks the forming bar. GPT's description of Claim 1 mechanism is imprecise — the real issue is tick-price vs. final-close evaluation of flip_check, not generic "state drift from reprocessing." The overwrite at lines 1386–1396 after a signal is a third bug GPT didn't identify.

The three-part failure chain: (1) flip fires on a transient tick price that would fail on bar close → spurious long; (2) 3-bar minimum dead zone before any reversal can cock; (3) if a qualified cross contradicts the signal direction at lines 1386–1396, the cocking permanently targets the wrong direction.
