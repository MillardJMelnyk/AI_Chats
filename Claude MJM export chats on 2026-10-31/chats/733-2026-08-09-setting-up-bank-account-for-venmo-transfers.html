what info do I need set up a bank account for cash transfers in venmo?
Claude2026-08-09 20:23 UTC

To link a bank account to Venmo, you typically need:

  1. Bank routing number (9 digits)
  2. Bank account number
  3. Account type (checking or savings)
  4. Name on the account (must match Venmo profile name)
  5. Venmo account verified — phone number, email, and identity verification (SSN or last 4 digits, date of birth) may be required first, especially for higher transfer limits

Venmo verifies the bank link via either:

  • Instant verification (login credentials for the bank, via Plaid) — immediate
  • Micro-deposit verification — two small deposits (under $1) appear in 1–3 business days; you confirm the amounts in the app

Note: requirements can shift — Venmo's current linking flow should be checked at help.venmo.com if precision matters for your case.
