How (status as of the latest reports found, March 2026): Take the letter to a Correos de México counter, buy international postage for USA, and hand it over; ask for "correo certificado" if you want tracking.

  • Letters: plain letters, postcards, and administrative, legal or academic papers with no commercial value are accepted, since service for these restarted on September 12, 2025.
  • Anything with goods inside: package service to the US restarted March 25, 2026, with a 10% US tax on declared value, a $800 cap, a 20 kg cap, and an itemized content and country-of-manufacture declaration. Medicines and herbal products are banned, and sender and recipient email and phone are required.
  • I found nothing indicating a new suspension after March 2026; Likely still current.

Closest outlet: I only have Tequisquiapan on file, not your street address. The Tequisquiapan post office is Guillermo Prieto 7, Centro, 76751, phone (414) 273 3003. Hours listed vary by source: Mon–Fri 8:00–16:30, Sat 10:00–14:00 per one, Saturday 8–12 per another — call before a Saturday trip. If you're not in Tequisquiapan proper, give me your address.
