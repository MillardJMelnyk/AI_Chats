I'm watching the movie train dreams. It doesn't look like it was shot for widescreen. Do you know why?
Claude2025-12-07 22:02 UTC

The film was deliberately shot in a 3:2 aspect ratio (approximately 1.50:1) rather than widescreen as an artistic choice.

Cinematographer Adolpho Veloso and director Clint Bentley chose this narrow, boxy format to evoke the feeling of old photographs and memories. They looked at historical photographs from the 1920s logging era and wanted the film to feel like "you're watching someone's memories" or "you found a box full of pictures."

The aspect ratio matches both vintage photographs and modern phone photos, intentionally creating a photographic/memorial quality rather than an epic cinematic look.
