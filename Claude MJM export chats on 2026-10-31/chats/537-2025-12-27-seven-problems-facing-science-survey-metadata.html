Article Metadata:

  • Title: "The 7 biggest problems facing science, according to 270 scientists"
  • Authors: Julia Belluz, Brad Plumer, Brian Resnick
  • Publication: Vox
  • Date: July 14, 2016
  • URL: https://www.vox.com/2016/7/14/12016710/science-challeges-research-funding-peer-review-process

Correction to premise: The survey did not specifically declare "peer-review broken." Peer review was one of seven identified problems. The survey asked: "If you could change one thing about how science works today, what would it be and why?" The seven problems identified were: (1) funding, (2) perverse incentives, (3) poor replication, (4) peer review flaws, (5) paywalls, (6) poor public communication, (7) academic careers being grueling.

Methodological note from original article: The authors explicitly stated the survey "was not a scientific poll" and respondents "disproportionately hailed from the biomedical and social sciences and English-speaking communities."
