I need to search for current Android screen recording apps to give you accurate, up-to-date information.

Top 3:

1. Built-in Android Screen Recorder (Android 11+)

  • Location: Quick settings toggle (swipe down notification shade)
  • Size: 0 MB (native to OS)
  • Features: Basic recording, audio source selection
  • Limitations: Minimal customization, no editing tools
  • Best if: You want zero bloat and basic recording is sufficient

2. AZ Screen Recorder

  • Size: ~12 MB
  • Features: One-tap recording, no watermark, no time limit, no root, records up to 1080p/60fps (free version), basic trim/edit tools, multiple stop methods (shake phone, screen off)
  • Limitations: Ads in free version, 60fps cap without premium (~$3-5 one-time)
  • Best if: You want the most popular/proven option with minimal setup

3. XRecorder (by InShot)

  • Size: ~15 MB
  • Features: No watermark, no time limit, records 1080p/60fps free, facecam option, paint tool while recording, basic editor, works offline
  • Limitations: Ad-supported
  • Best if: You want slightly more features than AZ (paint tool, facecam) at same simplicity level

All three require no root. AZ and XRecorder both have 4.6+ stars with 10M+ downloads each.

OnePlus 12 Pro screen recorder location:

  1. Quick Settings (primary method):

    • Swipe down twice from top of screen to expand Quick Settings
    • Look for "Screen recording" or "Screen record" tile
    • If not visible: tap the pencil/edit icon → drag "Screen recording" tile to active area

  2. Alternative if missing from Quick Settings:

    • Open any app (game, browser, etc.)
    • Swipe down once → tap the gear icon (Settings)
    • Search "screen record" or navigate to: Special features → Screen recording
    • Toggle it on

  3. Game Space method (if using games):

    • Open Game Space app
    • Launch a game → swipe in from side to open Game Tools
    • Screen recording option should be there

OnePlus 12 Pro runs OxygenOS 14 (Android 14), so the feature is definitely present. If you still can't locate it after checking Quick Settings edit mode, the tile may have been disabled—search "screen" in Settings app to find the toggle.

Okay how about a super simple video editor. All I really need to do is clip extraneous material from the beginning and endings of videos I've screen captured.
Claude2025-12-01 14:53 UTC

Top 3 (simplest first):

1. Google Photos (built-in on most Android devices)

  • Size: Already installed
  • Method: Open video → Edit icon → drag timeline handles → Save Copy
  • Limitations: Cannot remove middle sections, only trim ends
  • Best if: You want zero additional apps

2. VidTrim

  • Size: ~8 MB
  • Features: Trim ends, no watermark on basic trim (watermark only applies to advanced features like effects/transcoding), simple interface, ads are short
  • Limitations: Ads in free version, watermark if using advanced features
  • Best if: You want slightly more control than Google Photos but still dead simple

3. YouCut

  • Size: ~30 MB
  • Features: Cut/trim/split, no watermark, HD export, one-time purchase option (~$3) removes ads
  • Limitations: More features = slightly more complex interface than needed for your use case
  • Best if: You might want to split middle sections out (not just trim ends)

Recommendation: Try Google Photos first since it's already on your phone. If you need to remove middle sections, use VidTrim.

Give me concise simple steps to use Google photos.
Claude2025-12-01 15:01 UTC

Google Photos video trim steps:

  1. Open Google Photos app
  2. Tap the video you want to trim
  3. Tap "Edit" (pencil icon at bottom)
  4. Drag the white handles on the timeline to mark start/end points
  5. Tap "Save copy"

Done. Original video stays untouched, trimmed version saves as new file.

To find videos in Google Photos:

  1. Open Google Photos app
  2. Tap "Library" tab at bottom right
  3. Look for "Videos" section/album
  4. Tap it to see all videos

  • Videos are mixed with photos by date

If you still see nothing, your screen recordings may be saving to a different location. Check your phone's default Gallery app (the right icon in your screenshot) instead.
