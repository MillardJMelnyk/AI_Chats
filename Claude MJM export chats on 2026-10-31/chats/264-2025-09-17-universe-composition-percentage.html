Claude2025-09-17 20:03 UTC

Galaxy cluster containing over 1,000 galaxies, 330 million light-years away in constellation Coma Berenices.

Where Zwicky discovered dark matter evidence in 1933 - galaxies moving too fast for visible matter's gravitational binding.

Claude2025-09-17 20:04 UTC

Correct. Both posited to explain observations that didn't match existing theory.

Dark matter: Posited because galaxy velocities exceeded what visible matter could gravitationally bind.

Dark energy: Posited because universal expansion was accelerating when it should decelerate.

Neither directly observed - inferred from gravitational/expansion effects.
