Abasteo is likely a legitimate store, but there's almost no recent evidence about Abasteo itself; most of what exists is about its sister store, Cyberpuerta.

  • Abasteo itself: Trustpilot rates it 1.8 out of 5 from 15 reviews. That page is about 6 years old (around 2020), so it's a small, stale sample.
  • Ownership: a 2019 forum commenter says Abasteo is run by the same people as Cyberpuerta and recommends it for wholesale buying. That is a forum claim, not a company statement. Abasteo's own page describes itself as serving 30,000+ business customers, so it's mainly a business supplier.
  • Cyberpuerta (sister store): eKomi rates it 9.2/10 from 716 reviews in the last 12 months, with reviews as recent as Oct 23, 2026.
  • Main risk: another 2019 forum post says Cyberpuerta holds no inventory of its own; it lists wholesalers' live stock and cancels orders outright if that stock or the cost changes. If Abasteo works the same way, its P2584403 page, last indexed about 873 days ago, is likely to show the item available when it isn't, or get the order cancelled.
