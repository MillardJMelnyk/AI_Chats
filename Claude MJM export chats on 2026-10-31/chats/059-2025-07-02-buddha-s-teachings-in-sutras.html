fill in the blank: Jesus spoke in parables and Buddha spoke in __________.
Claude2025-07-02 18:16 UTC

Jesus spoke in parables and Buddha spoke in sutras.

Sutras are the traditional format for recording Buddha's teachings in Buddhist literature. They typically begin with "Thus have I heard" and contain the Buddha's discourses, dialogues, and teachings delivered to his disciples and followers. Like parables, sutras often use stories, analogies, and examples to convey deeper spiritual truths and moral lessons.

Claude2025-07-02 18:17 UTC

Jesus spoke in parables and Buddha spoke in koans.

Koans are paradoxical riddles or questions used in Zen practice to provoke enlightenment, though they're more associated with later Zen tradition than Buddha's original teachings.

If you want something even more universal, you could say Buddha spoke in allegories or teachings - both capture how he used stories and analogies to convey spiritual wisdom, similar to Jesus's use of parables.
