What is an ancient language that no one learns or speaks anymore
Claude2025-11-30 01:51 UTC

Hittite.

An extinct Indo-European language spoken in Anatolia (modern Turkey) circa 1600-1200 BCE. Deciphered in the early 20th century from cuneiform tablets but not actively learned or spoken by any living population.

What modern language is spoken by the fewest number of people
Claude2025-11-30 01:53 UTC

Taushiro - approximately 1 native speaker remaining as of early 2020s, Peru (Amazon region).

Alternative candidates with similarly critical status:

  • Kaʼapor Sign Language - 1-2 signers, Brazil
  • Lemerig - ~2 speakers, Vanuatu (2010s data)
  • Chemehuevi - ~3-5 speakers, USA (California/Arizona)

Exact current counts impossible to verify without recent field surveys. "Fewest" depends on timestamp and definition of "speaker" (native/fluent vs. semi-speakers).
