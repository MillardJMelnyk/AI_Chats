Here are some words and phrases that capture the "quality of representativeness":

Single words:

  • Representativeness
  • Typicality
  • Exemplarity
  • Authenticity
  • Fidelity
  • Accuracy
  • Validity

Compound terms:

  • Representative quality
  • Typical nature
  • Exemplary character
  • Faithful representation
  • True-to-form quality
  • Emblematic nature
  • Characteristic essence

More formal/academic terms:

  • Verisimilitude (truthfulness to reality)
  • Congruence
  • Correspondence
  • Isomorphism
  • Homomorphism
  • External validity (in research contexts)

Contextual variations:

  • In statistics/research: Generalizability, external validity, population representativeness
  • In art/media: Verisimilitude, realism, faithfulness
  • In democracy/politics: Proportionality, democratic representation
  • In samples/examples: Typicality, paradigmatic quality

The best choice depends on your specific context. "Representativeness" itself is often the most direct term, while "typicality" works well for everyday usage, and "fidelity" or "authenticity" might be better for creative or artistic contexts.
