The central operating principle that underlies every government is the monopoly on legitimate force within a defined territory. This concept, most famously articulated by sociologist Max Weber, means that governments are the sole entities authorized to use physical coercion—whether through police, military, courts, or other enforcement mechanisms—to maintain order and enforce laws.
