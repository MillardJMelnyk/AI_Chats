S-words shared by religious and military language:

  • Sacred/Sanctify/Sanctity
  • Sovereign/Sovereignty
  • Submission/Submit
  • Summons
  • Soldier (of Christ/of the nation)
  • Savior
  • Sword (of the Spirit/of the state)
  • Shield (of faith/of defense)
  • Standard (banner/flag in both)
  • Steadfast
  • Sworn (oaths in both domains)
  • Sentinel
  • Supremacy/Supreme
  • Shepherd (of souls/of troops)
  • Spiritual (spiritual warfare/esprit de corps)
  • Stronghold

What are words that mean to make a virtue out of some characteristic or habitual action
Claude2026-01-09 13:32 UTC

Words for making something virtuous:

  • Valorize
  • Glorify
  • Sanctify
  • Ennoble
  • Idealize
  • Romanticize
  • Lionize
  • Exalt
  • Elevate
  • Dignify
  • Venerate
  • Hallow
  • Consecrate
  • Enshrine
  • Apotheosize
  • Moralize (frame in moral terms)
  • Euphemize (when making vice sound virtuous)

Specific to turning negatives into positives:

  • Reframe
  • Spin (colloquial)
  • Whitewash (negative connotation)
  • Sugarcoat
  • Sanitize
