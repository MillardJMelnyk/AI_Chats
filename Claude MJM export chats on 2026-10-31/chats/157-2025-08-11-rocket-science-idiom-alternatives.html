what term currently in use corresponds to "rocket science" in expressions like, "This isn't rocket science!"
Claude2025-08-11 14:32 UTC

The modern equivalent expression is "This isn't brain surgery!"

Both "rocket science" and "brain surgery" are used interchangeably in English to mean something isn't extremely difficult or complex. They're both considered highly technical, demanding fields that require exceptional skill and precision.

You might also hear variations like:

  • "It's not rocket surgery!" (a humorous blend of the two)
  • "It's not neurosurgery!"
  • More casually: "It's not that hard!" or "It's not complicated!"

But "brain surgery" is definitely the most common alternative to "rocket science" when emphasizing that something should be straightforward or easy to understand.
