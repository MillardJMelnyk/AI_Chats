what's an expression that starts with "social _________" that means a paraiah?
Claude2025-08-14 20:48 UTC

The expression you're looking for is "social pariah" - which literally means someone who is avoided or rejected by society or a social group.

Another common similar expression is "social outcast" - someone who has been excluded from a social group or society.

Both terms describe someone who is shunned, avoided, or treated as an outsider by their community or social circle.

Claude2025-08-14 20:48 UTC

You're absolutely right! Since "pariah" already means someone who is avoided or rejected by society, adding "social" in front of it is redundant.

I think the expression you're looking for might be "social outcast" - which is a common way to describe someone who has been excluded or rejected by their social group or community.

Other similar expressions that start with "social" include:

  • Social exile
  • Social reject
  • Social leper (though this can be considered insensitive due to its reference to the disease)

"Social outcast" is probably the most commonly used and natural-sounding of these options.
