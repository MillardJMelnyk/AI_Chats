What is the Gaza tribunal
Claude2025-12-30 05:09 UTC

There are two distinct entities using this name:

1. The Gaza Tribunal (International)

  • Established: November 2024, London
  • Led by: Richard Falk (former UN Special Rapporteur on occupied Palestinian territories)
  • Model: Russell Tribunal tradition (civil society "people's tribunal")
  • Hearings: Sarajevo (May 2025), Istanbul (October 22-26, 2025)
  • Final verdict issued: October 26, 2025, concluding Israel is committing genocide in Gaza and that "Israeli perpetrators and their Western enablers" should not be allowed to escape justice
  • Status: Non-governmental; does not purport to determine guilt or liability of any person, organisation or state—issues "moral judgments," not legally binding rulings

2. The Gaza Tribunal (UK-focused)

  • Held: September 4-5, 2025, Church House, Westminster
  • Host: Jeremy Corbyn's Peace & Justice Project
  • Focus: UK's alleged complicity in Israeli war crimes, including evidence of British covert support for Israel, and whether the government's actions matched legal obligations to prevent genocide
  • Origin: On 4 June, Jeremy Corbyn MP introduced a bill into the UK House of Commons calling for an independent inquiry into UK involvement in Israeli military operations in Gaza. The government rejected this bill on 4 July.

Both are civil society initiatives without legal authority, operating in response to perceived failures of formal international justice mechanisms.
