What techniques does Lingopie use?
Claude2025-12-24 14:21 UTC

Based on search results:

Core Learning Features:

  • Dual subtitles (original language + English simultaneously)
  • Clickable words/phrases for instant translation
  • Video segments automatically saved with flashcards
  • Adjustable playback speed
  • AI-powered pronunciation coach

Practice Methods:

  • Video flashcards (includes clip where word appears)
  • Spaced repetition system
  • Quizzes and games
  • Voice recording for pronunciation practice
  • Daily challenges

Content Integration:

  • Netflix/Disney+ integration via browser extension
  • 5,000+ hours of TV shows, movies, music videos, podcasts, audiobooks
  • Content organized by difficulty level
  • Dialect filtering (e.g., different Spanish varieties)

Additional:

  • Live group lessons with instructors
  • Community access (Discord)
  • Progress tracking
  • Grammar tips displayed contextually
