Which empires/regimes/dynasties in history lasted for more than a few centuries? Simple list.
Claude2025-07-16 16:14 UTC

Here are empires, regimes, and dynasties that lasted more than a few centuries:

Roman Empire (27 BC - 476/1453 AD) - Western Roman Empire lasted ~500 years, Eastern Byzantine Empire lasted ~1000 years

Chinese Dynasties:

  • Han Dynasty (206 BC - 220 AD) - ~400 years
  • Tang Dynasty (618-907 AD) - ~300 years
  • Ming Dynasty (1368-1644) - ~275 years
  • Qing Dynasty (1644-1912) - ~270 years

Islamic Caliphates:

  • Abbasid Caliphate (750-1258) - ~500 years
  • Ottoman Empire (1299-1922) - ~620 years

European:

  • Holy Roman Empire (962-1806) - ~850 years
  • Venetian Republic (697-1797) - ~1100 years
  • Portuguese Empire (1415-1999) - ~580 years
  • Spanish Empire (1492-1898) - ~400 years

Other:

  • Pharaonic Egypt (various dynasties, ~3000 years total)
  • Japanese Imperial system (660 BC - present, though with varying actual power)
  • Ethiopian Empire (1270-1974) - ~700 years
  • Mughal Empire (1526-1857) - ~330 years
  • Khmer Empire (802-1431) - ~630 years
Millard2025-07-16 16:16 UTC
So Egypt was the only one to last much more than about 1 millennium.
Claude2025-07-16 16:16 UTC

Actually, that's not quite right. Several lasted much longer than a millennium:

Ancient Egypt - ~3,000 years (various dynasties from ~3100 BC to 30 BC)

Japanese Imperial system - ~2,600+ years (traditionally dated from 660 BC, still continuing today, though with varying degrees of actual power)

Venetian Republic - ~1,100 years (697-1797 AD)

Byzantine Empire - ~1,000 years (476/527-1453 AD, depending on how you count the transition from Western Rome)

So Egypt was the longest-lasting by far, but Japan's imperial system has technically been the most continuous (though it went through periods where emperors had little real power), and Venice lasted over a millennium as a republic. The Byzantine Empire just hit the 1,000-year mark.

Most other major empires did indeed fall in the few-centuries range, rarely exceeding 600-700 years of continuous rule.
