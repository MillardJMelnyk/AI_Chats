what are other words I could use instead of invocation: invocation: hallucinating and conjuring up conflict from nothing
Claude2025-10-08 15:46 UTC

Alternatives for "invocation" in your context (creating conflict from nothing):

  • fabrication (making up false conflict)
  • confabulation (unconsciously generating false narratives)
  • invention (creating what doesn't exist)
  • conjuration (summoning into existence, already in your phrase)
  • manufacture (producing artificially)
  • generation (bringing into being)
  • construction (building from scratch)
  • projection (imposing internal states onto external reality)
  • hallucination (already in your phrase, perceiving what isn't there)
  • confection (assembling artificially)
  • contrivance (devising artificially)

Most precise for your meaning: fabrication or manufacture (emphasis on falsity) or projection (emphasis on psychological mechanism).

Claude2025-10-08 15:46 UTC

Alternatives preserving the sorcery/magic allusion:

  • summoning (calling forth from nowhere)
  • conjuration (already in your phrase, but standalone option)
  • incantation (spoken spell creating effect)
  • evocation (calling into manifestation)
  • enchantment (casting spells to create illusion)
  • spellcasting (literal magical creation)
  • bewitchment (causing false perception through magic)
  • sorcery (direct magical manipulation)
  • hex (curse creating negative effect)
  • transfiguration (magically transforming nothing into something)

Most direct substitutes maintaining magical framing: summoning, evocation, or conjuration (standalone).
