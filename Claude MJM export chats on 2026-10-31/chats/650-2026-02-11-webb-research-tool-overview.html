Webb (thewebb.io) — Summary

Status: Pre-release / Early Access (as of data retrieved Feb 11, 2026)

Core Product

Webb is a local-first AI research tool designed to process documents, PDFs, notes, and data files entirely on the user's machine without cloud uploads.

Key Features (per official site)

  • 100% local processing — no data sent to external servers
  • Pattern recognition — identifies recurring themes across documents
  • Cross-reference engine — connects ideas between papers, notes, data sources
  • Timeline analysis — tracks concept evolution across research
  • Semantic understanding — context and meaning, not just keyword matching
  • Contextual synthesis — combines insights from multiple sources with citations
  • Memory systems — builds long-term understanding of research patterns
  • Executive summaries — generates briefs for large document collections
  • Visual canvas — spatial organization of insights, drag-and-drop for URLs/files
  • OCR capability — reads/analyzes text from photos

Creator

Ian Carroll (ethical hacker, security researcher, founder of Seats.aero) and two collaborators. Carroll is known for security vulnerability disclosures (hotel locks, TSA systems, automotive APIs, McDonald's hiring platform).

Naming

Named after Gary Webb, investigative journalist behind the "Dark Alliance" series on CIA-cocaine connections.

Licensing

Planned open-source release (~1 week after initial beta per Dec 2025 statements).

Integration

Can optionally plug in external LLMs (e.g., Grok) as additional processing layers.

Current Use Case Highlighted

Analysis of the Epstein document release (~1.5 million+ files from DOJ). Tool provides cited answers from ingested documents without hallucination.

Release Timeline (from Dec 2025 video)

  1. Free beta to journalists/content creators
  2. Beta testers who signed up
  3. General public
  4. Open-source release

Pricing

Not disclosed. Early access signup available on thewebb.io.

Related Product

Buyer App — grocery transparency app from the same team.
