Claude2025-12-03 16:42 UTC

There isn't a single recognized cultural name equivalent to "honor killing."

It was simply called "infanticide" or "child murder" — clinical terms that obscured the shame-driven motivation. Historians studying specific cases refer to it as "concealment infanticide" when the context was hiding illegitimacy, but this never gained currency as a cultural category.

That's the distinction: Islamic cultures openly named and discussed honor killing as a practice (even if condemned); Christian societies treated infanticide as individual criminal acts or sins, never as a culturally-sanctioned practice deserving its own category. The institutional suppression of discussion — calling it merely "murder" rather than "shame murder" — made it invisible as a system.

If you needed a parallel name for rhetorical purposes, "concealment infanticide" or "shame infanticide" would be accurate, but neither has historical recognition.
