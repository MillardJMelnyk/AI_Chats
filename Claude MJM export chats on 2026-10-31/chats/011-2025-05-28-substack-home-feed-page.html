The page you're referring to is typically called the Home or Feed page on Substack. This is the main dashboard you see when you log into your Substack account, and it displays:

  • Posts from newsletters you're subscribed to
  • Notes (short-form posts) from writers you follow
  • Comments and other activity from your network
  • Recommendations and discovery content

You can usually access this by clicking on the Substack logo or "Home" link when you're logged in. The exact layout and naming might vary slightly depending on whether you're using the web version or mobile app, but this central feed is where all the content from your subscriptions and network activity aggregates.
