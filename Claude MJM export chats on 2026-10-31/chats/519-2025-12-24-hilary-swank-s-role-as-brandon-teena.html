Brandon Teena was a trans man living in Falls City, Nebraska. The character was based on the real Brandon Teena (1972-1993).

Key facts:

  • Born female, lived as male
  • Dated Lana Tisdel
  • Discovered to be transgender by John Lotter and Tom Nissen
  • Raped by them on December 25, 1993
  • Murdered by them on December 31, 1993, age 21
  • Also killed that night: Lisa Lambert and Phillip DeVine

The film dramatized the final weeks of Brandon's life, focusing on the relationship with Lana and the events leading to the murder.
