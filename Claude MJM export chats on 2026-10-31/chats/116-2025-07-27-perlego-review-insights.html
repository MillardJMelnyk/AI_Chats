what do people say about Perlego, is it worth it?
Claude2025-07-27 20:21 UTC

Based on recent reviews, opinions about Perlego are generally positive but mixed. Here's what people are saying:

Positive feedback: Most customers feel their monthly payment was well worth it, as it saved them both money and time compared to dealing with hard copies in libraries or bookstores. Students particularly appreciate the cost savings - one user mentioned it "has saved me tons of money on textbooks for my classes" and found it great for research paper references without library visits.

The platform has a "fantastic range of books" with "great functionality" and subscription charges that are "incredibly reasonable" when considering textbook costs. On Trustpilot, Perlego maintains a 4-star rating based on over 2,300 reviews.

Negative concerns: Some users have technical complaints about "slow loading or not loading at all" with the app. There are also limitations that frustrate some users - you can "only view one chapter at a time, and after 10% of the book, you can no longer copy".

Bottom line: Whether it's worth it depends on your needs. For students and researchers who need access to many academic books and textbooks, most users find the subscription cost justified compared to buying individual books. However, if you need extensive copying capabilities or have poor internet connectivity, you might find the limitations frustrating. The service seems most valuable for people who read widely across multiple subjects rather than those who need deep access to just a few books.
