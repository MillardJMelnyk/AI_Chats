is there a way to get Firefox to sync bookmarks and history across devices like edge does?
Claude2026-08-26 21:55 UTC

Yes — Firefox Sync.

Setup: Firefox → hamburger menu → "Sign in to sync" (or Settings → Sync). Create/use a Firefox Account, sign in on each device, same account.

Data synced: bookmarks, history, open tabs, passwords, extensions, preferences — selectable per-category in Sync settings.

Platforms: Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS all supported.

Encryption: end-to-end; Mozilla can't read synced data.

Same paradigm as Edge sync — account-based, not device-pairing.
