A direct hit on Ixtapa is highly unlikely. The storm is Tropical Storm Vance, and it is moving away.

Status per NHC Advisory #21, 9:00 AM CST (1500 UTC) Mon Oct 26 2026:

  • Vance has weakened to a tropical storm. Its center was at 15.4N 105.7W, about 265 mi (425 km) SSW of Manzanillo, with maximum sustained winds of 65 mph (100 km/h), moving WNW at 7 mph (11 km/h).
  • That puts it about 510 km from Ixtapa (17.66N 101.55W). This is my own calculation from the coordinates.
  • No coastal watches or warnings are in effect.
  • The forecast track keeps moving it away. It continues WNW for about a day, then turns northwest on Tuesday and north on Wednesday. The forecast has it weakening to 40 kt and reaching 19.2N 108.4W by Wednesday 1200Z, becoming a remnant low by Thursday, and dissipating by Saturday Oct 31. That path ends up off Sinaloa, not Guerrero.
  • The heavy-rain areas forecast through Thursday are southern Sinaloa, southwest Durango, and coastal Nayarit, Jalisco, Colima and Michoacán. Guerrero is not on the list.

What does reach Ixtapa:

  • Surf. Swells from Vance are forecast to cause life-threatening surf and rip currents along the coast of southern, southwestern and west-central Mexico over the next few days.
  • Port closure. The Zihuatanejo port captaincy closed the port to navigation from 20:00 on Friday Oct 23. I found no reopening notice as of this search.

Why you may have heard otherwise: An AccuWeather page dated Friday Oct 23 showed Hurricane Vance affecting the area from Tuesday morning into late Wednesday night. That forecast is outdated. Since then, NHC has noted that the wind threat to southwest Mexico is decreasing as the track shifts westward away from the coast.

We had planned a vacation in Ixtapa from November 5th through 10th. What are the chances of bad weather there during that time ?
Claude2026-10-26 17:04 UTC

Low chance: November is the start of the dry season there, and no storm is forecast. Your dates are beyond the reach of any real forecast, though.

Tropical storm risk: Unlikely

  • As of 5:00 AM PDT Mon Oct 26 2026, NHC expects no new tropical cyclone to form in the eastern Pacific over the next 7 days. That covers the period through about Nov 2, three days before you arrive.
  • Vance is forecast to have dissipated by Oct 31, off Sinaloa.
  • Nov 5–10 is 10–15 days away. No agency issues storm-formation forecasts that far out, so the window between Nov 2 and Nov 10 has no official coverage yet.
  • One caution: this season has been unusually active. NHC's eastern Pacific energy index is 245.0, the second-highest on record after 1992. I did not retrieve historical data on how often storms hit Guerrero in early November, so I can't put a number on that risk.

Ordinary rain: Likely minor The average-weather sites disagree, so here is the range from the two that look credible:

Source November rainy days November rainfall
WeatherAPI 4 39 mm (1.54 in)
weather-stats.com 1 0.9 in (about 23 mm)
  • One other site claims about 26 rainy days in November. That figure contradicts every other source and should be ignored.
  • From those averages, my rough calculation is a 1-in-30 to 4-in-30 chance of rain on any given day. Over your 6 days, that works out to about an 18% to 57% chance of at least one rainy day. This treats each day as independent, which real weather isn't, so take the range as approximate.

When it becomes knowable:

  • NHC's 7-day outlook first reaches Nov 5 around Oct 29 and covers your whole stay from about Nov 3.
  • Ordinary local forecasts become reliable roughly 5–7 days ahead, so around Oct 30 to Nov 1.

Ciclón tropical: poco probable

  • El NHC no espera que se forme ningún ciclón en el Pacífico oriental en los próximos 7 días, es decir, hasta el 2 de noviembre aproximadamente (aviso de las 5:00 AM PDT del 26 de octubre).
