In other words: creating space to invoke socialized psychosis is the point.

There is no “innocent until proven guilty” because questions of truth—the question of what’s really going on—are matters of fact, not matters of morality, of innocence or guilt.
The problem in supremacist, authoritarian cultures like ours is that these distinctions are so occulted and confused, people believe and act like there are no distinctions to make. In the rare cases where we can’t figure out if and who is lying, we’re left to do no worse than what we already do now with a “hung jury”: hold to our own opinions based on our own best judgment.
