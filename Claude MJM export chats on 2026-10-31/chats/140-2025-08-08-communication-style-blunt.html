Current phrases for praise/admiration:

"Gas up" - give compliments to boost confidence "Slay" - doing something exceptionally well
"That's fire" or "it's fire" - really good/impressive "No cap" - I'm being honest/real (when praising) "Shout out" (SO) - public acknowledgment/praise

"Gas up" seems most common for direct praise equivalent to your examples.
