how do I see my viewing history in facebook, i.e., the posts I've viewed in my news feed?
Claude2025-07-27 16:37 UTC

Facebook doesn't provide a way to view your complete news feed viewing history - there's no built-in feature that shows you all the posts you've scrolled past or viewed in your feed.

However, you can access some related information:

Your Facebook Activity:

  • Go to your profile and click "Activity Log"
  • This shows posts you've interacted with (liked, commented, shared) but not just viewed

Off-Facebook Activity:

  • Settings & Privacy → Settings → Your Facebook Information → Off-Facebook Activity
  • This shows some browsing data but not your news feed viewing

Download Your Information:

  • Settings & Privacy → Settings → Your Facebook Information → Download Your Information
  • You can download your data, which includes some activity logs, but this still won't show your complete viewing history

The closest thing to what you're looking for would be your "Recent Ad Activity" (in Ad Preferences), which shows some posts you've interacted with recently, but it's limited and ad-focused.

Facebook likely doesn't offer this feature due to privacy concerns and the sheer volume of data it would represent. Your news feed viewing history would be massive and potentially sensitive information.
