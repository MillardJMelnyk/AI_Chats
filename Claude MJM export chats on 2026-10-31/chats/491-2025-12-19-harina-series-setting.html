US (FBI UCR): Counts crimes reported to police Mexico (INEGI ENVIPE): Victimization survey capturing both reported and unreported crimes

In Mexico, 93.2% of crimes are not investigated (cifra oculta/dark figure) — meaning ENVIPE captures approximately 14× more crime than police reports would show.

Raw numbers for reference:

Jurisdiction Rate per 100,000 Source/Method

US National 2,119 FBI UCR (police reports)

To approximate comparable figures:

If you applied Mexico's ~93% underreporting rate to US FBI data, US cities would show rates roughly 14× higher. Conversely, if you looked only at crimes reported to police in Mexico (~7% of total), CDMX would show ~3,800 per 100,000 — comparable to mid-range US cities.

The US also publishes the National Crime Victimization Survey (NCVS), which is methodologically closer to ENVIPE. That survey shows the violent crime victimization rate rose from about 16 per 1,000 people in 2020 to 22.5 in 2023 — or 2,250 per 100,000 for violent crime alone.

Bottom line: The comparison you're asking for cannot be made accurately without normalizing for reporting methodology. Do you want me to search for NCVS city-level data, which would be more comparable?
