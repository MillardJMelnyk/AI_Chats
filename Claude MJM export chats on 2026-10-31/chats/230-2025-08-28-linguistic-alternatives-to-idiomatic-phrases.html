baldface lie or baldfaced lie?
Claude2025-08-28 19:23 UTC

Baldfaced lie is correct.

"Baldface" exists only as a noun (describing animals with white facial markings) or adjective referring to those markings.

"Barefaced lie" is also correct and more traditional - meaning shameless/undisguised.
