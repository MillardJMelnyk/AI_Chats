CONTRACT FOR SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT SERVICES E-Commerce Platform – Gym Membership System

THE SERVICE PROVIDER [FULL NAME OF PROVIDER] RFC: [PROVIDER'S RFC] CURP: [PROVIDER'S CURP] Address: [FULL ADDRESS] Email: [EMAIL ADDRESS] Phone: [PHONE NUMBER]

THE CLIENT [FULL NAME OF CLIENT] RFC: [CLIENT'S RFC] CURP: [CLIENT'S CURP] Address: [FULL ADDRESS] Email: [EMAIL ADDRESS] Phone: [PHONE NUMBER]

In the city of [CITY], State of [STATE], on the [DD] day of [MONTH], [YEAR], the individuals named above, hereinafter referred to collectively as "The Parties," agree to enter into this Contract for Software Development Services (hereinafter "the Contract"), pursuant to the following:

DECLARATIONS AND CLAUSES

I. DECLARATIONS

I.1 THE SERVICE PROVIDER declares:

  • That they are a natural person with full legal capacity to enter into this contract.
  • That they possess the technical knowledge, experience, and tools necessary to provide the services covered by this contract.
  • That they have no legal impediment to binding themselves under the terms of this instrument.

I.2 THE CLIENT declares:

  • That they are a natural person with full legal capacity to enter into this contract.
  • That they have an interest in contracting the software development services described herein.
  • That they have sufficient financial resources to meet the payment obligations agreed upon herein.
  • That they have no legal impediment to binding themselves under the terms of this instrument.

CLAUSE ONE – OBJECT OF THE CONTRACT

THE SERVICE PROVIDER agrees to design, develop, and implement an e-commerce platform for gym membership management across multiple locations (hereinafter "the Platform"), with the following technological architecture:

Component Technology Description
Frontend Nuxt (Vue.js) Custom, fast, SEO-optimized user interface
CMS Strapi Management of content, images, and marketing materials
Backend E-commerce Bagisto (Laravel) Memberships, checkout, and customer accounts
Payment Processing Openpay Secure, tokenized payment processing

The services include three working environments: development, staging (pre-production), and production, as well as server configuration, version control with Git, and deployment of the Platform in the production environment.

CLAUSE TWO – SCOPE OF SERVICES

The scope of services THE SERVICE PROVIDER is obligated to deliver comprises exclusively the following:

  • Design and development of the frontend with Nuxt (Vue.js).
  • Configuration and integration of Strapi as the content management system.
  • Installation and configuration of Bagisto for membership management, shopping cart, and customer accounts.
  • Integration of the Openpay payment gateway with tokenized processing.
  • VPS server configuration with Nginx, SSL, DNS, PM2, and database.
  • Implementation of a staging environment for client review.
  • Deployment to the production environment.
  • Post-launch validation.

Expressly EXCLUDED from this contract are: ongoing maintenance, future updates, new features, post-launch technical support, and any other service not described in this clause, unless separately agreed upon in writing via addendum to this instrument.

CLAUSE THREE – PRICE AND PAYMENT TERMS

The Parties agree that the total price for the services covered by this contract is $50,000.00 MXN (Fifty Thousand Pesos 00/100), payable according to the following schedule:

Payment Description Amount When Due %
Advance Project start $25,000.00 MXN Upon signing this contract 50%
Second payment Staging delivery $15,000.00 MXN Upon written client approval 30%
Final payment Production deployment $10,000.00 MXN Prior to production launch 20%

Payments shall be made via electronic transfer to the bank account designated in writing by THE SERVICE PROVIDER, or by any other means agreed upon by The Parties. In the event of late payment on any installment, THE CLIENT shall pay a late interest charge of 3% (three percent) per month on the outstanding balance, without requiring prior judicial or extrajudicial notice. THE SERVICE PROVIDER may suspend work until the outstanding payment is resolved.

Third-party operating costs (hosting, domain, Openpay) are the exclusive responsibility of THE CLIENT and are not included in the price agreed upon in this clause.

CLAUSE FOUR – EXECUTION TIMELINE

THE SERVICE PROVIDER agrees to perform the services within an estimated period of [XX] calendar weeks from receipt of the advance payment established in Clause Three. This timeline may be modified due to causes attributable to THE CLIENT, including delays in providing materials, information, access, approvals, or timely payment of installments. Any modification to the timeline must be documented in writing and accepted by both Parties.

CLAUSE FIVE – OBLIGATIONS OF THE SERVICE PROVIDER

  • Execute the services described in Clause One with diligence and professionalism.
  • Maintain ongoing communication with THE CLIENT regarding project progress.
  • Deliver the staging environment for client review and approval prior to production deployment.
  • Implement security measures in accordance with industry best practices.
  • Maintain confidentiality of client information pursuant to Clause Ten.
  • Use version control systems (Git) to allow rollback in the event of critical failures.
  • Transfer ownership of the source code and configurations to the client upon receipt of full payment.

CLAUSE SIX – OBLIGATIONS OF THE CLIENT

  • Make timely payment of installments in accordance with the schedule in Clause Three.
  • Provide in a timely manner all information, materials, access, and resources necessary for project development.
  • Review and approve the staging environment, or request adjustments, within no more than [10] business days of delivery.
  • Contract and directly pay for all third-party services required for Platform operation (VPS hosting, domain, Openpay).
  • Designate a representative or point of contact with decision-making authority for the duration of the project.
  • Not disclose to third parties any technical information, methodologies, or processes of THE SERVICE PROVIDER without their written consent.

CLAUSE SEVEN – INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND TRANSFER OF RIGHTS

Once THE CLIENT has paid the full price agreed upon in Clause Three, THE SERVICE PROVIDER shall transfer full ownership of the following elements developed specifically for this project:

  • All frontend and backend source code and infrastructure configurations.
  • Digital content and media produced for the Platform.
  • Technical documentation generated for the project.
  • Properly configured staging and production environments.

Third-party software components integrated into the Platform (Bagisto, Strapi, Nuxt/Vue.js, Openpay, and others) are governed by their respective open-source or commercial licenses, and their use is subject to the terms and conditions of those providers. THE SERVICE PROVIDER does not transfer rights to third-party software.

During the term of the contract and until full transfer, THE SERVICE PROVIDER retains rights over the work in progress. Payment default by THE CLIENT suspends the transfer obligation.

CLAUSE EIGHT – WARRANTY AND ERROR CORRECTION

THE SERVICE PROVIDER provides a warranty of [30] calendar days from the date of production deployment, during which any errors or failures in Platform functionality directly attributable to the development work performed will be corrected at no additional cost. This warranty does not cover:

  • Modifications, configurations, or alterations made by THE CLIENT or third parties not associated with THE SERVICE PROVIDER.
  • Failures arising from third-party services (hosting provider, Openpay, etc.).
  • New features or scope changes requested after delivery.
  • Errors caused by improper use of the Platform.

CLAUSE NINE – LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

THE SERVICE PROVIDER shall not be liable for loss of revenue, data, customers, or profits arising from the use or inability to use the Platform, nor for failures, interruptions, or deficiencies in third-party services contracted by THE CLIENT. THE SERVICE PROVIDER's maximum liability under this contract shall be limited to the total amount effectively paid by THE CLIENT as of the date of the claim.

CLAUSE TEN – CONFIDENTIALITY

The Parties agree to maintain strict confidentiality over all information exchanged in connection with this contract, including technical, commercial, financial, and business information. This obligation shall remain in effect for a period of [2] years following termination of the contract, regardless of the cause of termination. Information that is in the public domain or that must be disclosed by order of a competent authority is excepted.

CLAUSE ELEVEN – SCOPE MODIFICATIONS

Any modification, expansion, or reduction of the scope of services described in Clause Two must be agreed upon in writing via an addendum signed by both Parties, specifying any additional cost or reduction and any corresponding adjustment to the delivery timeline. Verbally requested scope changes shall create no obligation for THE SERVICE PROVIDER.

CLAUSE TWELVE – EARLY TERMINATION

THE CLIENT may rescind this contract at any time by providing [15] calendar days' written notice. In such case, THE SERVICE PROVIDER shall be entitled to retain the advance payment and to claim the proportional value of work completed up to the date of rescission, without liability to THE SERVICE PROVIDER.

THE SERVICE PROVIDER may rescind the contract in the event of payment default by THE CLIENT for more than [15] calendar days past the due date of any installment, or in the event of conduct that prevents the normal progress of work. In this case, THE SERVICE PROVIDER shall retain all payments received as a contractual penalty.

CLAUSE THIRTEEN – CONTRACTUAL PENALTY

If THE CLIENT fails to meet any payment obligation set forth in Clause Three and does not remedy the default within [15] calendar days of the due date, THE CLIENT shall pay THE SERVICE PROVIDER, as a contractual penalty, an amount equivalent to [10%] of the unpaid installment, without prejudice to the collection of late interest charges provided in Clause Three.

CLAUSE FOURTEEN – PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION

The Parties agree that the processing of personal data arising from this contract shall be conducted in accordance with the Federal Law on Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (LFPDPPP) and its regulations. Personal data of the Platform's end users shall be handled in accordance with the privacy notice implemented by THE CLIENT, who shall be the data controller with respect to said users. End-user payments shall be processed directly by Openpay, and THE SERVICE PROVIDER shall not store card data or any other sensitive payment information.

CLAUSE FIFTEEN – RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN THE PARTIES

This contract does not create any employment relationship between The Parties or between THE CLIENT and any collaborator or subcontractor that THE SERVICE PROVIDER may engage. THE SERVICE PROVIDER acts as an independent service provider and is solely responsible for their own tax, social security, and labor obligations with respect to their collaborators, pursuant to the Income Tax Law, Social Security Law, and other applicable regulations.

CLAUSE SIXTEEN – GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

This contract is governed by the provisions of the Federal Civil Code, the Commerce Code, and other applicable laws of the United Mexican States. For the interpretation and performance of this instrument, The Parties expressly submit to the jurisdiction of the competent courts of the city of [CITY], [STATE], waiving any other jurisdiction that might apply by reason of their present or future domiciles.

CLAUSE SEVENTEEN – GENERAL PROVISIONS

  • This contract constitutes the entire agreement between The Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes any prior verbal or written agreement on the same matter.
  • If any clause is declared invalid or unenforceable, the remaining clauses shall remain in full force and effect.
  • Tolerance by one Party of the other's non-compliance does not constitute a waiver of the right to demand compliance in the future.
  • Notices between The Parties shall be delivered by email to the addresses set forth in the header of this instrument, unless otherwise agreed.
  • The Parties agree that advanced electronic signatures or digitized handwritten signatures have full evidentiary validity pursuant to the Commerce Code.

SIGNATURES OF CONFORMITY

Having read this instrument and being fully informed of its content and legal scope, The Parties sign it in the city of [CITY], [STATE], on the [DD] day of [MONTH], [YEAR].
