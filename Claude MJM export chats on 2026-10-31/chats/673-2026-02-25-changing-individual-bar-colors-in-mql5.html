Changing individual bar colors in MQL5

Created 2026-02-25 15:31 UTC · Last updated 2026-02-25 15:47 UTC · 4 messages

Can you change the color of just one bar on an MT5 chart using MQL5 code?
Claude2026-02-25 15:31 UTC

Wednesday, February 25, 2026, 12:00 PM CST

Yes — but only through a custom indicator, not by modifying the built-in candlestick/bar rendering directly.

The mechanism: use DRAW_COLOR_CANDLES or DRAW_COLOR_LINE (or similar DRAW_COLOR_* drawing styles) in a custom indicator. These styles give you a color index buffer where you assign a per-bar color index. Set one bar's color index differently from the rest, and that single bar renders in a distinct color.

You cannot selectively recolor a bar on the chart's native price series without overlaying a custom indicator that redraws the bars.

will candles drawn that way scale with the screen as you zoom in and out?
Claude2026-02-25 15:47 UTC

Yes. DRAW_COLOR_CANDLES draws from the same OHLC buffer data and is rendered by the chart engine itself — not as graphical objects. They scale with zoom exactly like native candles.

The standard approach is to hide the native chart candles (ChartSetInteger(0, CHART_COLOR_CANDLE_BULL, clrNONE), etc.) and let your indicator redraw all bars, coloring the target bar differently. Visually seamless.
