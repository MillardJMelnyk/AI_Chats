Debit card vs credit card:

Same issue applies. The restriction is not credit-card-specific — it's an AML (Anti-Money Laundering) policy that applies to all card transactions:

  • You deposit $1,000 via debit card
  • You grow account to $3,000
  • You can only withdraw $1,000 back to that debit card (as a "refund")
  • The remaining $2,000 profit must go to a verified bank account

The "DO NOT HONOR" rejections were the user's issuing bank refusing the refund transaction, not the broker blocking it. This can happen with either debit or credit cards depending on bank policies.

Practical recommendation: Fund via bank wire from the start. Withdrawals return the same way with no routing complications. Both IC Markets and Pepperstone charge ~$20 for international wire, but it eliminates the card-related friction entirely.
