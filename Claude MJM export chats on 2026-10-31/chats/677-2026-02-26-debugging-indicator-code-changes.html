Bug 1: isNewBar wipes confirmed provisional's buffer arrows, then backstop re-fires on the same bar

This is the primary live-bar bug. The sequence:

  1. Live tick N: provisional signal (e.g., LONG) fires on bar X. Buffer arrow set, object arrow created.
  2. New bar arrives: isNewBar = true, rates_total increments. Lines 1464–1474 confirm the provisional on bar X. g_lastSignalBar = X, g_isCocked = false.
  3. The signal loop starts at signalStart = X (i.e., prev_calculated - 1), processing both bar X and bar X+1.
  4. Line 1531–1535: Because isNewBar is true, BufferArrowBuy[X] and BufferArrowSell[X] are blanked to EMPTY_VALUE. The confirmed signal's buffer arrows are destroyed.
  5. Bar X is re-processed through signal logic. Since g_isCocked was reset to false at line 1470, no MA trigger fires. But a raw cross on that bar hits the backstop at line 1886–1890, which bypasses blackout (line 1907–1908). A backstop signal fires — potentially in the opposite direction from the confirmed provisional.
  6. Result: orphaned object arrow from the original LONG + new SHORT arrows from the backstop, or vice versa. Both directions on the same bar.

Root cause: The arrow-clear at lines 1531–1535 doesn't protect the bar that was just confirmed, and the backstop has no awareness that the bar already has a committed signal.
