Based on search results, here's how to get your MT5 credentials for The5ers contest:

Where to find your credentials:

  1. The5ers Hub Dashboard – Log into hub.the5ers.com. The dashboard contains "Trading Platform Credentials" where you can easily find your login details for MT5.

  2. Email – Once you join a contest/account, you receive the credentials immediately via email and The5ers Hub.

To connect MT5:

Open your MT5 platform and use the login (account number), password, and server info from your Hub dashboard or email. The server for The5ers is typically accessed via their branded MT5 instance.

If you haven't received credentials:

After joining a contest in the Hub (Sidebar → "Contests" → selecting and applying to a contest), once your application is successful, you'll choose your operating system and download the trading platform – credentials should then be accessible.

Still missing?

Check spam/junk folders for the email. If nothing appears in Hub or email, contact The5ers support directly – the search results indicate their documentation doesn't provide a standalone setup guide, suggesting credentials are delivered via Hub/email after registration completion.
