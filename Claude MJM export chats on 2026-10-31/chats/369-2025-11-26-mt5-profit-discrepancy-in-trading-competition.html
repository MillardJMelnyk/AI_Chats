I'm doing a 5ers.com trading competition. The profit on the trade (MT5 "Toolbox" Trading tab) is $19.95 more than on the balance line. These would normally be the same for a FOREX broker live account. What is that difference supposed to be?
Claude2025-11-26 23:24 UTC

I need current 5ers.com fee structure. Let me search.

That $19.95 is 5ers' trading commission of $4 per lot round trip. Your $19.95 difference indicates you opened/closed approximately 5 standard lots on that trade—roughly $4 × 5 lots = $20.

The "Toolbox" Trading tab shows gross P&L. The balance line shows net P&L after commissions are deducted. This is normal for 5ers; they extract their commission on all forex trades.

Millard2025-11-26 23:25 UTC
That's kind of ironic. The competition is for a funded account. So they "charge" a commission when it's their own money?
Claude2025-11-26 23:25 UTC

Yes. It's their own capital, yet they extract value via both commission ($4/lot) and profit split (80-100% to you, 0-20% to them depending on program).

Most prop firms use one mechanism or the other. 5ers uses both—which means they're compensated whether you're profitable or not (commissions), plus they participate in your wins (profit share).

You could frame it as: they're charging for capital access, platform infrastructure, and liquidity. Or you could frame it as redundant fee extraction. Either way, it's baked into their model.
