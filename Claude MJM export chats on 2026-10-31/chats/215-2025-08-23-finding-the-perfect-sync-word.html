Here are some short words that convey alignment, agreement, or high resonance:

Single syllable:

  • Sync - suggests perfect alignment or harmony
  • Match - indicates correspondence or agreement
  • Click - implies instant connection or resonance
  • Mesh - suggests fitting together well
  • Vibe - indicates resonance or harmony

Two syllables:

  • Attuned - suggests being in harmony or alignment
  • Aligned - direct term for being in agreement or sync
  • Cohered - indicates sticking together in agreement

Sync is probably the most concise and widely understood option, capturing that sense of perfect alignment or being "in sync" with something or someone.
