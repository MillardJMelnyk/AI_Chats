IFLScience (originally "I Fucking Love Science") is a science communication website and social media brand founded by Elise Andrew in 2012. It began as a Facebook page sharing viral science content—images, articles, videos—and expanded into a standalone website.

Key characteristics:

  • Targets general audiences with accessible, often sensationalized science content
  • Heavy emphasis on shareability over depth
  • Covers astronomy, biology, health, environment, technology
  • Owned by IFL Science Ltd (UK-based)

Criticism:

  • Frequently criticized by scientists and science journalists for clickbait headlines, oversimplification, and occasional inaccuracy
  • Has been accused of misrepresenting research findings for engagement
  • Not a primary source; aggregates and rewrites content from press releases and other outlets

Utility: Acceptable for casual science awareness; unreliable as a citation for rigorous claims. Always trace to the original peer-reviewed source if using anything from IFLScience.
