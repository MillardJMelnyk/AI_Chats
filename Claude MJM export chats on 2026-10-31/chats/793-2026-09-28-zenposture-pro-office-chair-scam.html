It's mixed. The chair itself gets decent marks. The company gets bad ones.

The chair:

  • TechRadar (an established tech site) tested it and called it a quality chair at an approachable price. It has a lot of adjustments and a sturdy frame. Complaints: armrests move too much, and the footrest is loose.

  • Trustpilot: 1.8 out of 5 from 42 reviews. Complaints are about slow delivery, bad customer service, and refund problems.
  • One reviewer says their 3-star review was never published and suspects the good reviews are filtered.

Bottom line: it's a real, usable chair, but not a bargain at $500. Buy it on Amazon rather than direct from HBADA, since Amazon returns are easier if something goes wrong.
