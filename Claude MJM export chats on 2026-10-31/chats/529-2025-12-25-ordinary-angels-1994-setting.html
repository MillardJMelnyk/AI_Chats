How many of the details about getting Michelle to the hospital in the movie were accurate?
Claude2025-12-25 20:36 UTC

Based on the search results, here's the breakdown:

Accurate to the real events:

  • A liver became available requiring transport to Omaha, Nebraska with only hours to get there before the organ was no longer viable
  • When Michelle's grandmother answered the transplant call at 9 a.m., she was told a liver would be waiting by sundown, needing to reach Omaha by 6-7 p.m.
  • A snowstorm on Jan. 16-17, 1994 resulted in a single-day snowfall record of more than 15 inches, with temperatures plunging to -22 degrees by Jan. 19
  • Sharon used radio to mobilize support after receiving Barbara Schmitt's call
  • A helicopter was brought to Southeast Christian Church parking lot, and around 200 Louisville residents volunteered to shovel the snow
  • The helicopter took Michelle to Standiford Field where she was transported by private jet to Omaha

Inaccurate/Changed:

  • Michelle was only three when her family got the call, not five as shown in the film
  • The locals plowed through the snow during the day instead of at night as portrayed
  • The movie states 700 miles to the hospital; the actual distance by plane is 582 miles

The core transport mechanics—helicopter to church parking lot, community snow clearing, private jet to Omaha—were accurate. Timing and Michelle's age were altered.
