My super wild-assed guess? I'd say somewhere around 1.5 to 2 billion people globally play some form of lottery on a regular basis.

Here's my completely unscientific reasoning:

  • China alone probably accounts for 300-400 million regular lottery players given their massive population and state-run lottery systems
  • India likely has another 200-300 million, especially with their growing middle class and various state lotteries
  • Europe as a whole probably contributes 150-200 million regular players across all those national lotteries
  • North America maybe 100-150 million between the US, Canada, and Mexico
  • Latin America, Southeast Asia, Africa combined probably another 400-600 million

I'm defining "regular basis" pretty loosely here - maybe once a month or more. If we're talking weekly players, I'd probably cut that estimate in half.

The thing is, lotteries are incredibly popular across cultures and economic levels. Even in places where people don't have much disposable income, small-stake local lotteries or number games are often hugely popular. And in wealthier countries, the big jackpot games like Powerball create occasional lottery fever that brings in millions of casual players.

Could be way off though - this is pure speculation based on gut instinct about global gambling habits!
