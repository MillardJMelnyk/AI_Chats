WTF is he talking about, "the oil, land, and other assets that they previously stole from us" ?? Venezualans stole their land from us?
Claude2025-12-19 15:32 UTC

Trump's apparent reference: Venezuela's 1970s nationalization of its oil industry, when the government took control of oil operations from foreign (including American) companies.

Historical facts:

  1. Pre-1970s: US oil companies (Exxon, others) operated in Venezuela's sovereign territory extracting Venezuelan oil under concession agreements with the Venezuelan government.

  2. 1976: Venezuela nationalized its oil sector, creating state company PDVSA. This was Venezuela exercising sovereignty over its own natural resources on its own land.

  3. Not "stolen from US":

    • The oil was always Venezuelan oil in Venezuelan territory
    • Nationalization = ending foreign concessions, not theft of US property
    • Standard practice globally (Mexico 1938, Iran 1951, many others)

Recent context:

  • 2019 (Trump's first term): US imposed sanctions effectively blocking PDVSA oil exports
  • 2022: Biden granted Chevron limited operating license
  • March 2025: Trump revoked it, then reissued with conditions

Trump's claim has no legal/historical basis. Sovereign nations nationalizing their own natural resources on their own territory ≠ "stealing" from foreign corporations or the US government.

His language suggests either: (a) genuine belief US companies' lost profits = theft, or (b) rhetorical framing to justify military action and resource seizure.
