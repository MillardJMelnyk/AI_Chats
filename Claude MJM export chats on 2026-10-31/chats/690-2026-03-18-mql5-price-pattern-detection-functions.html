Resolution modes

  • ANY_AGREEMENT — any single enabled method firing is enough. Most trades, most opportunity, most noise.
  • MAJORITY — more than half of enabled methods must agree on direction. Balances selectivity and frequency.
  • UNANIMOUS — all enabled methods must agree. Fewest trades, highest conviction only.
  • REQUIRED_PLUS_ANY — one designated method must fire AND at least one other must agree. Lets you say "pullback must always be present, plus anything else confirms it."

Signal resolution logic per bar

  1. Evaluate each enabled method → returns +1, -1, or 0
  2. Count long votes, short votes, total enabled methods
  3. Apply resolution mode → net direction or 0
  4. If net direction differs from current position → fire signal via HandleSignalEvent
  5. Apply exit rules independently on each bar regardless of entry method
