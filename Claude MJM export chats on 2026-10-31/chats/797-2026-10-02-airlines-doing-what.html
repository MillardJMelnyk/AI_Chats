None. The claim is a recurring hoax (2021 origin, recycled in 2022).

  • IATA said it is not aware of any airlines banning vaccinated passengers or considering it.
  • Lead Stories found no publicly available evidence of airlines banning the vaccinated; the same "Airlines to Ban Vaccinated After Pilots Die" title appeared in a July 2022 Rumble video.
  • Full Fact and others found no record of airline meetings on the topic.
  • Experts found no evidence of an added blood-clot risk for vaccinated air travelers.
  • The British Airways pilot deaths cited as the trigger were not tied to the vaccine in any news coverage.

My search returned only 2021–2022 sources, so I found nothing confirming a 2026 development.
